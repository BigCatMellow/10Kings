# Decisions

## Status

**Active. Writing reference, not setting canon.** This page owns the rulings on the Sunday Morning work: what the author has decided, what was decided in the work and still waits for his reading, and what is open for him. Quotations are the author's words from the working conversations. **Only the D-rows bind;** W-rows are reversible until the author reads them. Build on these rather than re-deriving them ([the author's watch-list](Craft.md#the-authors-watch-list): "pulling on resolved threads"). A decision is changed only by the author; record the change here with its date, then update the page it applies to.
