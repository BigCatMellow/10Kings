# The Mushrooms From Nowhere

## Status

**Provisional story concept.** Written with the [Sunday Morning framework](../Notes/Sources/Framework.md). All characters, places, customs and plot details are new and non-canon. Promoted from [Story Seeds](Story-Seeds.md).

**Development level: L2 Planned** (2026-11-02). One of seven stories added inside the collection's year at bt 7274's request ([D16](../Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)). THINK and PLAN are recorded below; there is no draft yet.

## Premise

A mushroom grower from an Underpass enclave climbs the stair to sell at a pass town's late-summer fair, because the man who used to carry her crop up lost his corridor in the spring shake. She hasn't stood under the sky since she was a girl. Everyone assumes her mushrooms come from Deepwood. In a blight year that makes them either a miracle or stolen.

**Setting:** the late-summer fair at Ninewell Saddle, the pass town of [The Road in Use](The-Road-in-Use.md). Below it is Underwell, a small Underpass junction enclave around a spring (provisional).
**Clock:** the late-summer fair, Year 2 late summer, over three fair days. A fair, not a festival ([Calendar #20](../../Calendar-and-Timekeeping.md#20-mobile-circuits-are-calendars-in-motion)).
**Mode:** a fish-out-of-water comedy.

## Protagonist

**Xenna Dree**, a grower from Underwell.

- **Good at:** growing. She reads damp and air by feel and knows a bed is turning before it shows. Below, she can drive a hard bargain.
- **Small flaw:** she distrusts the surface and everyone on it. She assumes she's being laughed at, and she won't say where she's from. Below, you don't tell surface people your roads.
- **Why she resists:** she never wanted to sell up here. That was the broker's job, and she'd like it to be his job again.

## Place

The same saddle town in high summer: two gates, the animal yards, the fair spread along the cleared blessing road, and the stair-hut by the east gate, open again.

Recurring locations:

1. **The stair-head**, where the dark ends and the light is like a slap.
2. **The fair road**, stalls along the saddle, the warden's table at the gate.
3. **The warden's table**, with its fair book and its marks.
4. **The animal yards**, where Zan Coyle rents mules and doesn't talk.
5. **A borrowed corner of the fair road**, no stall, just crates and a cloth.

## Cast

- **Warden Galt**, the fair's market warden. He's a stickler and a decent one. This year he has a rule for forest goods and no rule for anything else.
- **Fitch**, a cheerful dealer with a Deepwood house's mark and an easy offer.
- **Zan Coyle**, the mule-wrangler from [The Road in Use](The-Road-in-Use.md), and **Vinegar**. He talks about as much as Xenna does.
- **Old Ondine**, a herb-seller up from the Twilighthollow side for the fair. She has handled forest mushrooms all her life and can tell you in one sniff what these aren't.
- **Fairgoers**, who are curious, suspicious, and hungry for mushrooms.

## Problem

In a blight year the fair has a rule against stolen and spoiled forest goods: mushrooms must carry a Deepwood house's mark. Xenna's carry none, because they aren't from Deepwood, and nobody has written a rule for anything grown below. If she can't sell, Underwell can't buy its winter oil and grain. Fitch will put his house's mark on her crates for half the price and sell them as forest mushrooms.

## Complications

1. The light. She can't see properly for a day, and her hat makes everyone think she's Deepwood.
2. Every "where are they from?" gets "below", which sounds like a joke or a lie.
3. Warden Galt can't let unmarked mushrooms be sold, and he's right to worry.
4. Fitch's offer is easy, and the winter is real.
5. People ask whether the blight can reach cellar beds. She says her beds are clean. Whether they'll stay clean, she can't say, and nobody can.

## Running elements

- **The hat**, a grower's wide brim against lamp-glare below and sun-glare up here. Everyone reads it as a Deepwood hat. By the end she's taken it off.
- **"Below."** Her whole answer to every question about where.
- **The sky**, which she keeps looking at like it's about to fall.

## Emotional core

Recognition for people whose work travels through networks nobody sees. Being seen is a risk, and it's sometimes the only way to be paid fairly.

## Solution

A choice. Xenna turns down Fitch and takes a named stall. She tells Warden Galt the enclave's name, Underwell, and her own, and nothing about the roads. Galt makes a new line in the fair book for goods grown below, with her name on it. The mushrooms sell as what they are.

## Climax

The warden's table on the second evening. To get a line in the fair book she has to say where she's from out loud, with half the fair listening. Ondine has already told anyone who'll listen that these aren't forest mushrooms, they're cellar mushrooms, and better than anything she's seen at a fair this year. That doesn't make the choice for her. It just means someone's on her side when she makes it.

## Soft landing

The third day. She sells out. Zan's mules carry oil and grain to the stair-head, and neither of them says much. The final beat is her at the stair-head with the hat in her hand, taking one last look up before she goes down.

### Stage 0 — Add the story

Promoted 2026-11-02 from the seed *The Mushrooms From Nowhere* ([Story Seeds](Story-Seeds.md)). The seed's "for the first time in thirty years" is changed to "since she was a girl", because "for thirty years" is a [stock phrase](../Notes/Registry.md#stock-phrases-to-avoid). Material brief: Underpass route wars, a Domino Map gap. Shape targets against the neighbors in reading order (Walkout Pies before, The Blank Square after): a resolution by a choice; a fish-out-of-water register; an outsider who is the expert in her own work.

### Stage 1 — THINK

**Reasoning allocation:** structured single path. The risk is a polite antagonist who would echo the collection's land agent ([devices](../Notes/Registry.md#devices-already-used)), so the nail is a rule and a cheerful dealer instead.

**Decomposition.**

1. Why she has to come up.
2. Why the fair can't buy.
3. The easy way out.
4. What she has to give up to sell honestly.
5. The blight question.
6. Who helps without deciding for her.

Connection to keep: what she gives up (4) is her habit of secrecy (the flaw), and it costs her only the enclave's name, never its roads.

**Assumption mapping.**

| Assumption | Status | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Underpass grows fungi for food | `VERIFIED` | [The Underpass](../../Places/The-Underpass.md#economy). |
| A pass town has an Underpass stair | `ASSUMED` | Provisional. Deepwood has possible entrances, and the Spine has hidden routes. This one is planted in The Road in Use. |
| A late-summer fair exists here | `VERIFIED` in kind | A late-summer fair on a circuit ([Calendar #20](../../Calendar-and-Timekeeping.md#20-mobile-circuits-are-calendars-in-motion)). This fair is provisional. |
| A blight-year mark rule | `ASSUMED` | Provisional, a sensible local response. |
| The blight reaches cellar fungi | `UNKNOWN` | Left open. She speaks only for her own beds, this season. |
| Underpass enclaves have names surface people know | `ASSUMED` → **false** | Nobody at the fair has heard of Underwell. That's the point. |

**First principles.** The story needs a stranger with good goods, a rule that blocks her for good reasons, a dishonest shortcut that isn't villainous, and a choice that costs her something. Removed conventions: the villain buyer, and the market master as obstacle. Galt helps as soon as she gives him something to write. The smallest mechanism: a fair book with no line for her.

**Counterexample search.**

| Attack | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Why doesn't she just take Fitch's deal? | **Survives.** It sells her crop as something it isn't. If it's found out, Underwell can't sell here again. And she's proud of what she grows. |
| Is Ondine the resolution? | **Narrows.** She vouches before the choice, but she can't give Xenna a line in the book. Xenna still has to say the name. |
| Does naming Underwell betray the enclave? | **Narrows.** A name is not a road. She says nothing about how to get there. |
| Is this The Heavy Scale (a pass town, a stickler, a rule)? | **Risk.** There, an inspector finds a fault. Here, a stranger asks to be counted. Different protagonist, engine and resolution. |
| Is Zan's cameo too much? | **Narrows.** Two scenes, few words. The two people who don't talk is the joke, and it helps her stay. |
