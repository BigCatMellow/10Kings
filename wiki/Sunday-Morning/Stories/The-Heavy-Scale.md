# The Heavy Scale at Icestep Summit

## Status

**Provisional story concept.** Written with the [Sunday Morning framework](../Framework.md). All characters, businesses and plot details are new and non-canon.

## Premise

The public scale at Icestep Summit has been reading heavy all winter. A newly posted, painfully precise weights inspector has three days before the first caravan of the season to find out why.

**Setting:** [Icestep Summit](../../Places/Border-Towns.md#icestep-summit--northwind--highridge) (Northwind / Highridge)
**Clock:** [Pass Opening and Ice Breaking](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#seasonal-calendar), early spring
**Mode:** mystery

## Protagonist

**Anselm Quill** — a Highridge-trained weigher on his first posting.

- **Good at:** measurement, records, and remembering exactly what people said.
- **Small flaw:** quotes people back to themselves, word for word, at the worst possible moment.
- **Why he resists:** he wants to report the fault and let someone senior handle it. There is no one senior until the pass opens.

## Place

A cold pass town where maritime supply routes meet plateau caravans. Everyone is waiting for the snow to go.

Recurring locations:

1. **The public scale** — the town's honor, beam and pans under a slate roof.
2. **The customs house** — ledgers, stamps, one stove.
3. **The Second Gate** — Brisa Holloway's inn, where stranded travelers winter.
4. **The animal yards** — empty, then suddenly not.
5. **The repair shop** — gruff smith, every tool the pass needs.
6. **An Infinite Compass road shrine** — where the keeper knows everyone's comings and goings.

## Cast

- **Brisa Holloway** — Northwind-born innkeeper who feeds everyone snowed in, paying or not.
- **A salt-fish trader** — tags every claim by its source: *I saw it*; *someone I'd lend a boat to told me*; *it is said.*
- **The customs chief** — wants no scandal whatsoever.
- **The repair smith** — grumpy, obvious suspect, entirely innocent.
- **The shrine keeper** — pilgrim-host and quiet historian of who went where.
- **A caravan master** (arrives late in act II) — has been overcharged and intends to dispute every toll on the plateau.

## Problem

If the scale is wrong, every toll taken this winter is wrong. At Pass Opening the first caravan will weigh in publicly; if the reading is disputed, the town's scale — and its reputation on the route — becomes a joke told in every yard from here to the lowlands.

## Complications

1. Every interview adds a contradictory account, each carefully tagged by how the speaker knows it.
2. The customs chief wants the matter "resolved quietly," meaning not at all.
3. Quill's suspicion lands on the repair smith, who is rude, capable and innocent.
4. The pass opens early; the first caravan will arrive a day ahead of schedule.
5. Quill starts to doubt his own reference weights and spends a night re-weighing them against each other.

## Running elements

- The trader's evidential tags ([Northwind languages](../../Culture/Language-and-Thought.md#northwind-languages)).
- Quill quoting people exactly ([Highridge languages](../../Culture/Language-and-Thought.md#highridge-languages)).
- Brisa feeding Quill every time he interviews her, so every interview gets longer.
- The shrine keeper's travel gossip, most of it irrelevant and all of it accurate.

## Emotional core

Honesty and kindness can coexist. Quill learns that precision can be care instead of armor, and that a small public institution holds a surprising amount of trust.

## Solution

Innocent. In the worst storm of winter, the scale's counterweight was borrowed to hold down the Second Gate's roof. It was replaced with a Northwind net-stone that "looked the same." Nobody lied — everyone involved said exactly what they had seen, heard or been told.

## Climax

Quill corrects the scale publicly as part of the Pass Opening ceremony, with Brisa's embarrassed confession and the caravan master watching. The town decides — by shouting — to repay the winter's overcharged tolls as a free first-caravan supper at the Second Gate.

## Soft landing

Quill's report reads: *Error inspected and corrected. Cause: weather.* The supper runs late: [Icefire Brined Fish](../../Culture/Recipes/Border-Fusions.md#icefire-brined-fish), [Frost and Spice Fish Cakes](../../Culture/Recipes/Border-Fusions.md#frost-and-spice-fish-cakes), and caravan flatbread. The trader tells the story that night, tagged *I saw it.*
