# Inspected, Not Guaranteed

## Status

**Provisional story concept.** Written with the [Sunday Morning framework](../Framework.md). All characters, businesses and plot details are new and non-canon. Wurdren's biography is not settled by anything here.

## Premise

Wurdren brings the sword he has carried since his youth to Stonefield Forge to be re-hilted. The forge fires are cold for winter maintenance, so he is stuck for a week — and the district guild drafts him as the required "unbound" judge for the apprenticeship judgments, because he belongs to no guild at all.

**Setting:** [Stonefield Forge](../../Places/Border-Towns.md#stonefield-forge--ironcrest--greenvale) (Ironcrest / Greenvale)
**Clock:** [Forge Reawakening](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#ironcrest), early spring
**Mode:** character / competition

## Protagonist

**Wurdren** — see [Wurdren](../../Story/Wurdren.md).

- **Good at:** listening to people who are not usually listened to; noticing who is about to be left out.
- **Small flaw:** wants badly to be needed, and tells the story of his sword more often than anyone wants to hear it.
- **Why he resists:** he came to get a hilt fixed, not to sit in judgment over teenagers' metalwork in a town he doesn't know.

## Place

A mixed farming-and-craft town where the foothills meet farmland. Smoke, water and timber are old arguments; half the town repairs what the other half grows with.

Recurring locations:

1. **Tamsin Rake's shop** — cold hearth, swept floor, every tool hung by its outline.
2. **The Quench** — a tavern where forge crews cluster near the fire and talk over one another.
3. **The guild hall** — judgment benches, and the memorial wall of names honored at Forge Reawakening.
4. **The charcoal road** — the muddy route the relighting fuel has to come down.
5. **Nell Haskett's farmyard** — where the valley's broken hinges, gates and plowshares seem to collect.

## Cast

- **Tamsin Rake** — an old smith who will not let anything leave her shop merely *attempted*. Dry, exact, secretly kind.
- **Col Barrowfield** — an apprentice with a Greenvale mother and an Ironcrest father. His masterwork is a set of plowshare points and a pump fitting, not a blade or a lock.
- **Master Oswin Vey** — the district guildmaster. Overly serious, runs judgments by the book, and keeps a ledger of smoke complaints he is losing.
- **Nell Haskett** — farmer, town gossip, and unofficial census-taker of everything broken in the valley.
- **The Quench's keeper** — has heard every forge story ever told and believes none of them, including Wurdren's.

## Problem

The judgments need three judges, one of whom must hold no guild tie. The usual unbound judge is stuck on a washed-out road. Without a third judge the judgments slip past Forge Reawakening, and Col's year of work waits another season.

## Complications

1. Vey objects to an outsider, then decides an outsider is at least *neutral*, then regrets it.
2. Col's piece is dismissed by the other judges as "farm work," not craft.
3. The farmers threaten to block the charcoal carts on relighting day over smoke from the forges.
4. The charcoal is late anyway — the road is mud.
5. Tamsin inspects Wurdren's legendary sword and finds it is a middling blade, *guaranteed* decades ago by a smith who lied.

## Running elements

- Ironcrest's four-way distinction — *attempted*, *completed*, *inspected*, *guaranteed* — applied to everything, including the porridge. See [Language and Thought](../../Culture/Language-and-Thought.md#ironcrest-languages).
- Wurdren's sword story, which gets shorter every time he tells it.
- Nell's running tally of which farms owe Col a favor.
- Vey's smoke ledger, which gains a new page every scene.

## Emotional core

Competence deserves respect even when nobody sings about it. Col's useful, unglamorous work mirrors the career Wurdren is quietly disappointed in.

## Climax

Wurdren does not win the room with a speech. On judgment day, the farmers — who were threatening the charcoal carts — walk into the guild hall and lay the tools Col repaired over the year on the judgment bench as evidence: *inspected* work, in daily use. Col's pump fittings turn out to be exactly what the farms need to keep their water clear of forge runoff, which ends the smoke dispute too.

## Soft landing

The carts arrive, the fires relight, and names are read at the memorial wall. Tamsin re-hilts Wurdren's sword and stamps it *inspected*, not *guaranteed*: a fine, ordinary sword. He eats [Forged Harvest Stew](../../Culture/Recipes/Border-Fusions.md#blacksmiths--forged-harvest-stew) at The Quench and stays two extra days for no stated reason. Nell has a gate that sticks.
