# Story Seeds

## Status

**Provisional premise bank.** One-line Sunday Morning premises not yet developed with the full [writing template](../Notes/Sources/Framework.md#23-writing-prompt-template). Names and details are non-canon.

When a seed is developed, give it its own page in this folder and list it in the [folder index](../README.md).

Two seeds were promoted to story pages on 2026-11-02: [The Mushrooms From Nowhere](The-Mushrooms-From-Nowhere.md) and [The Ladder Rules](The-Ladder-Rules.md). Their seed text stays below as the record of where they started. The other two are `PARKED`: interesting, not yet given resources. Each carries a possible world thread; when a seed is developed, its thread goes through the pipeline's [world-tie check](../Notes/Pipeline.md#world-tie-check). Status labels follow the [Story Pipeline](../Notes/Pipeline.md#stage-5--reconcile-keep-the-records-honest).

The [world seeds](#world-seeds-2026-11-04) below were brainstormed on 2026-11-04 at bt 7274's request, to use more of the world. All of them are `PARKED` until he picks.

## World seeds, 2026-11-04

*bt 7274, 2026-11-04: "maybe we look at the world and brainstorm … how we can use the various aspects of it in stories to make sure we are using the dominos as well as fleshing out the world. I guess not every story has to progress the storys dominos or main plot, some can just be part of the world at large." That last sentence is [D23](../Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions).*

### How these were made

The brainstorm used the THINK pattern from bt 7274's [Pilot Projects](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/Pilot_Projects/blob/main/ai-creativity-and-ideation/README.md) research: generate widely first, keep the different ideas apart, judge them separately, then plan.

1. **Split the world into families** from the [World coverage](../../../atlas/coverage/Coverage.md) report and his examples: the Spine above and below, Seven Hill, Port, moving peoples, faiths and relics, remembered history, guilds and the shadier trades, how life works.
2. **Generate in each family** with a few different operations, so the seeds don't all come out the same shape: a point of view no story has used (perspective shift), the expected thing not happening (inversion), two parts of the world meeting (recombination), a season or scale moved (constraint shift).
3. **Keep the families apart.** Forty-odd ideas came out; thirty-one are kept here because each works a different way. Near-duplicates were dropped, not averaged.
4. **Judge separately** against the [Rules](../Notes/Rules.md) and the [Registry](../Notes/Registry.md#story-shapes) (the [table](#how-they-compare) below), then pick a few for different reasons rather than by one score ([picks](#picks)).

Nothing in a seed is canon. Where a seed needs something the wiki doesn't have, its **Needs** line says so, and nothing is assumed.

### Two kinds

- **World**: part of the world at large ([D23](../Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)). No nail, no domino. Current events can show as weather, or not at all. Every other rule holds.
- **Domino**: carries a nail and goes through the [world-tie check](../Notes/Pipeline.md#world-tie-check) as now.

A few seeds note where **Wurdren** could pass through, as the signal ([D19](../Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)). Those are Domino stories by definition.

### The Spine, above and below

#### The Walker's Stone

`WORLD` · *the [Overpath](../../Places/The-Spine.md#the-overpath) · late summer*

A Greenvale farmer who has never been above the tree line walks a stretch of the Overpath to carry up a stone her late husband always meant to carry. Everyone she meets on the trail is walking it for a different reason, and none of them is the reason she expected.

Possible heart: you can finish someone else's walk without it being a sad thing.

- **The everyday strange:** walkers' customs on the trail, treated as ordinary by everyone but her.
- **Needs:** who walks the Overpath, and whether it has a custom like carrying a stone (both [open](../../Places/The-Spine.md#the-overpath)). The sad moment is the husband, said once.

#### Above

`WORLD` · *the [Overpath](../../Places/The-Spine.md#the-overpath), over the [Underpass](../../Places/The-Underpass.md) · early autumn*

The daughter of an Underpass guide family knows every tunnel by the sound of its water. She goes up onto the Overpath for the first time with a party of walkers and is no use at all in open air, until the weather turns and she can hear the stream under the snow that nobody else can.

Possible heart: what you know is worth something even where it doesn't look like it.

- **Uses:** Underpass [guide families](../../Places/The-Underpass.md#politics) and [families born underground](../../Places/The-Underpass.md#settlement).
- **Needs:** the Overpath, as above.

#### The Air Keeper

`DOMINO` · *an inhabited junction in the [Underpass](../../Places/The-Underpass.md) · any season*

The woman who tends a junction's air (which shafts are open, where the bad-air pockets are this year) is retiring, and her apprentice has one week left to learn the thing she has never written down.

Possible heart: some work is only noticed when it stops.

Possible thread: a [collapse has redirected trade](../../Story/Current-Events.md#route-wars) through the junction, so there are more people breathing its air than it was kept for. [Mira Stonebridge](../../Story/Character-Roster.md#mira-stonebridge--underpass) could be one of the guides bringing them, as an ordinary person.

- **The everyday strange:** the [strange animal activity](../../Places/The-Underpass.md#current-events) the junction reads as weather.

#### The Deep Door

`DOMINO` · *an [Underpass](../../Places/The-Underpass.md) enclave · after a quake*

A quake opens a sealed space below the enclave. The enclave leader and a mining boss come down to look at it on the same day, and the enclave's cook has to feed both parties at one table, because there is only one table.

Possible heart: a shared meal is still a shared meal, even between people who want different things from the dark.

Possible thread: [quakes revealing sealed spaces](../../Story/Current-Events.md#earthquakeland-movement) and new mineral finds. [Ilya Dravencrest](../../Story/Character-Roster.md#ilya-dravencrest--underpass-enclave-leader) and [Gharic Coalveil](../../Story/Character-Roster.md#gharic-coalveil--underpassmining-interests) appear as ordinary people; nobody decides the door's future on the page.

- **Needs:** nothing new. What is behind the door stays shut or ordinary.

#### What the Pass Is Called

`WORLD` · *a reopened pass on the [Spine](../../Places/The-Spine.md) · spring*

A quake reopens an old pass, and the towns at either end need a name for the new route board. The name is [Old Spine speech](../../Culture/Language-and-Thought.md#language-families) nobody speaks; one town translates it "the Kind Gate" and the other "the Hungry Gate".

Possible heart: a place can mean two things and still be one place.

- **Uses:** Old Spine names that "each side of a border translates differently".
- **Needs:** nothing new. The two readings are invented for the seed.

### Seven Hill

#### Muster Bell

`WORLD` now, `DOMINO` later · *[Seven Hill](../../Places/Seven-Hill.md) · any season*

The town still rings the old base's muster bell once a year, and everybody turns out, but now it means a potluck on the old parade ground. This year the bell cracks, and the town has to decide whether a bell that only calls a supper is worth recasting.

Possible heart: a town keeps what it was, by making it into what it is.

- **Later:** a Season 4 [Return](../Notes/The-Long-Road.md#kinds-of-story) where the bell rings for a real muster, told from the same parade ground.
- **Needs:** where Seven Hill is, whose base it was and when it stopped ([open](../../Places/Seven-Hill.md#still-open)), and whether it has a bell.

#### The Walls Come Home

`DOMINO` · *[Seven Hill](../../Places/Seven-Hill.md) · summer*

For two generations families have been quietly carting stone off the old base walls to build houses and pens. Now a [builders' guild](../../Politics/Guilds.md#builders-and-infrastructure-guilds) contract wants the stone that's left, and every family has to admit which wall is in its kitchen.

Possible heart: a town is built out of what it used to be.

Possible thread: builders seeking "long contracts and legal control over standards"; the contract's buyer stays `UNKNOWN`.

- **Needs:** Seven Hill, as above.

### Port

#### The Harbor Runner

`WORLD` · *[Port](../../Places/Port.md) · Tide Return, early spring*

A Port-born kid who runs messages for the dock clerks speaks four kinds of Port and none of them the way the clerks want. A message goes astray between two quarters on the busiest morning of the year, and she has to get it back by asking in all four.

Possible heart: a city is a place where everyone half understands each other, and that's enough.

- **Uses:** [Port culture](../../Places/Port.md#culture) and native Port speech, [Trade Tongue](../../Culture/Language-and-Thought.md#language-families), [local delivery](../../Couriers-Posts-and-Message-Networks.md#4-local-delivery-is-its-own-problem), Tide Return ([festivals](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#seasonal-calendar)).
- **Needs:** nothing new. bt 7274's example: "a story of a kid in Port".

#### The Bucket Race

`WORLD` · *[Port](../../Places/Port.md) · summer*

Port's quarters compete every year in a fire-bucket race, and one quarter has never won. A kid from that quarter, too small for the line, works out that the race is really about who passes to whom.

Possible heart: a city that can't agree on anything still has to agree on the fire.

- **Uses:** Port's [fire response and watch](../../Places/Port.md#governance-and-protection), its [mixed families](../../Places/Port.md#culture).
- **Needs:** the race is a story-level custom.

#### The Yellow Flag

`WORLD` · *[Port](../../Places/Port.md) harbor · late autumn*

A ship sits out its quarantine in the harbor, and the woman who rows food out to it every day ends up carrying more than food: a game across the water, messages, one birthday.

Possible heart: kindness at the length of an oar.

- **Uses:** Port's [quarantine rules](../../Places/Port.md#governance-and-protection), [Medicine and Healing](../../Medicine-and-Healing.md).
- **Needs:** what Port's quarantine is like, kept vague; nobody on the ship is badly ill on the page.

#### Lean Vigil

`DOMINO` · *a poor [Port](../../Places/Port.md) district · Lean Vigil, midwinter*

A cook in a crowded district keeps one pot going all night for the watch, and this year there are more people in the district than there is pot.

Possible heart: a neighborhood's size is however many the pot can stretch to.

Possible thread: [refugee and worker pressure](../../Story/Current-Events.md#refugeeworker-pressure) on poorer districts; the newcomers' reasons stay their own.

- **Uses:** [Lean Vigil](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#port), the [Port charter and watch](../../Places/Port.md#governance-and-protection), [Port cuisine](../../Culture/Food.md#port).

### Moving peoples

#### Mending Day

`WORLD` · *a Greenvale village · late summer*

Once a year a circuit family of menders comes through, and the whole village has saved its broken things for them all year: pots, chairs, a clock, a fiddle. This year the old mender isn't with them, and his grandson has to do the lot.

Possible heart: being expected somewhere is a kind of home.

- **Uses:** [Nomads](../../Culture/Nomads.md): circuits, itinerant repair, "strong attachment to places even within a mobile life".
- **Needs:** nothing new. The sad moment is the old mender, said once.

#### The Stopping Place

`DOMINO` · *a Highridge spring on an old circuit · spring*

A circuit family arrives at the spring where they have stopped every spring for longer than anyone can say, and finds a new fence and a new owner who has never heard of them.

Possible heart: old custom is only as strong as the people who remember it.

Possible thread: land changing hands ([land and seed politics](../../Story/Current-Events.md#land-and-seed-politics)); the buyer's reasons stay ordinary.

- **Uses:** [Nomads](../../Culture/Nomads.md)' "traditional stopping rights, springs, pasture agreements".
- **Wurdren:** could be camped with them; "adventurers share roads, camps, repairs" with mobile people.

#### The Slow Boat

`WORLD` · *a [Sunplains](../../Regions/Sunplains.md) canal · Wine Crush, early autumn*

A canal boat family carries pickers and grapes down to the presses at Wine Crush, and one picker, who has worked every crush for twenty years, has decided this is her last.

Possible heart: you can love a season without loving the work.

- **Uses:** river and boat communities and seasonal agricultural labor ([Nomads](../../Culture/Nomads.md)), [Wine Crush](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#seasonal-calendar), [Sunplains cuisine](../../Culture/Food.md#sunplains).

#### The Ballad Line

`WORLD` · *a circuit camp · winter*

A circuit's ballads keep track of everyone: who married whom, who drowned, who ran off. A young woman wants her own name in one, and the old singer says a ballad only takes people when they've done something worth a verse. She's out to prove her wrong.

Possible heart: the stories a people keep about itself are kind and harsh at once.

- **Uses:** [Nomads](../../Culture/Nomads.md)' ballads as "archives of people, crimes, disasters, loves, wars, and places", and "humor and storytelling as social weapons".
- **Needs:** keep the song device in mind ([Registry](../Notes/Registry.md#devices-already-used)): three stories already run on a song or call.

### Faiths and relics

#### The Lantern

`WORLD` · *a village shrine · any season*

The village's relic lantern dims when the most respected woman in the village speaks at her granddaughter's betrothal. Nobody knows whether it meant anything, and the village has a week to decide what it meant.

Possible heart: what a thing means is up to the people who keep it.

- **Uses:** [Magic and Relics](../../Magic.md): real but deniable; "a relic that 'fails' a respected person causes a crisis". The story never confirms it.
- **Needs:** whose faith keeps the lantern.

#### Under the Lights

`WORLD` · *a [Northwind](../../Regions/Northwind.md) coast · Aurora Nights, late summer*

Two families have planned a betrothal under the lights, and the lights don't come. The grandmothers disagree about whether it counts.

Possible heart: a promise doesn't need the sky's permission.

- **Uses:** [Aurora Nights](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#northwind) (beliefs stay beliefs), [Northwind cuisine](../../Culture/Food.md#northwind).
- **Needs:** nothing new. Aurora Nights is the only festival no story has used.

#### The Wrong Day

`DOMINO` · *a Greenvale village · late summer*

The village's [Cyclic Order](../../Politics/Religions.md#1-the-cyclic-order) calendar-keeper finds that the festival calendar has drifted a day over forty years. Putting it right would move a wedding.

Possible heart: being right isn't always the same as being on time.

Possible thread: the Cyclic Order's reformers, who say stability has become stagnation, would love a drifted calendar. One of them visits; the keeper's reasons stay her own.

- **Uses:** [Calendar and Timekeeping](../../Calendar-and-Timekeeping.md).

#### Forge Reawakening

`DOMINO` · *an [Ironcrest](../../Regions/Ironcrest.md) foundry town · Forge Reawakening, early spring*

At the spring relighting, apprentices are judged on one piece each. An [Eternal Forge](../../Politics/Religions.md#4-the-eternal-forge) master has to judge her own nephew's, and it's good, and it's not his.

Possible heart: honesty in the work is the work.

Possible thread: Eternal Forge institutions "becoming active in labor and quality disputes"; the town is near Coalglen, a year after [Walkout Pies](../Drafts/Walkout-Pies.md).

- **Uses:** [Forge Reawakening](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#ironcrest), [Ironcrest cuisine](../../Culture/Food.md#ironcrest).

#### The Kitchen Court

`DOMINO` · *a border village three valleys from a magistrate · autumn*

The nearest court is three valleys away, so the village's [Harmonious Path](../../Politics/Religions.md#7-the-harmonious-path) households settle quarrels over supper. Tonight it's two neighbors and a goose, and the goose is at the table.

Possible heart: fairness tastes better with food.

Possible thread: Harmonious communities "building parallel mutual-aid and arbitration systems … unintentionally creating political power". A visitor writes down how well it works.

- **Needs:** keep the goose clear of [The Goat File](The-Goat-File.md).

#### The Same Sin

`WORLD` · *a [Dual Flame](../../Politics/Religions.md#8-the-dual-flame) counseling house · winter*

An old man confesses the same small sin to a young counselor every week. She sets out to find out why.

Possible heart: some people need to be listened to more than forgiven.

- **Needs:** the Dual Flame's counselors are seeing patterns across regions; this story stays clear of that, or it breaks the [world-tie rule](../Notes/Rules.md#the-world-tie-rule-connected-not-driven).

### Remembered history

#### The Name on Both Walls

`WORLD` · *a Greenvale / Ironcrest border town · Ember Remembrance, late autumn*

One man's name is cut on both towns' memorial walls, because both sides claimed him. His great-granddaughter is asked to read it at both remembrances on the same night.

Possible heart: a person can belong to two stories and be true in both.

- **Uses:** the [Ember Wars](../../History/Contested-Memory.md#the-ember-wars--the-burning-years) and their three memories, [Ember Remembrance](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#ironcrest)'s names on memorial walls, [cross-regional marriage](../../Social-Fabric.md#mixed-communities-create-local-culture).
- **Needs:** nothing new. Follow the [writing rule](../../History/Contested-Memory.md#writing-rule): "my grandfather called it…".

#### The Treaty Stone

`DOMINO` · *two farms on a Convergence line · after a flood*

A flood moves the named stone the Convergence used as the border between two farms. Both families know exactly where it was, and they disagree by eleven paces.

Possible heart: neighbors measure each other in more than paces.

Possible thread: the Convergence drew borders by "named stones", which "creates later ambiguity"; a land agent wants to buy whichever side wins.

- **Uses:** [border recognition](../../History/The-Convergence.md#border-recognition), [arbitration](../../History/The-Convergence.md#arbitration).
- **Needs:** "an object" as the resolution has been used ([Registry](../Notes/Registry.md#story-shapes)); this one shouldn't resolve by the stone.

#### Salt Between Them

`DOMINO` · *a convoy ship · Season 2*

A Northwind deckhand and a Sunplains cook on the same convoy grew up on opposite songs about the [Salted Coast](../../History/Contested-Memory.md#the-salted-coast-disputes): burned boats on one side, "northern pirates" on the other. They have to share a galley for six weeks.

Possible heart: you can't hate someone who keeps your soup warm.

Possible thread: [convoy politics](../../Story/Current-Events.md#piracy-and-convoy-politics) one rung up from [The First Fish](../Drafts/The-First-Fish.md).

#### The Seizure Warehouse

`WORLD` · *a [Port](../../Places/Port.md) warehouse · winter*

A Port family and a Northwind family have shared one warehouse for three generations, and each believes the other is only renting. The lease turns up.

Possible heart: who owns a place matters less than who's kept it.

- **Uses:** the [Port Seizure](../../History/Contested-Memory.md#the-port-seizure), remembered as occupation by one family and as theft by the other.
- **Needs:** an old document is a resolution to avoid ([Registry](../Notes/Registry.md#story-shapes)): the lease should settle nothing.

### How life works

#### The Midwife's Road

`WORLD` · *a border farm · a storm, early spring*

A midwife rides through a storm to a farm three hours out, and the father, who has already delivered two lambs that night, is sure he could have managed.

Possible heart: knowing when to call someone is a skill too.

- **Uses:** [Medicine and Healing](../../Medicine-and-Healing.md) (midwives, household care), with no magical healing.
- **Needs:** nothing new. Mother and baby are fine.

#### The Coat

`WORLD`, or a Thread later · *four regions · a year*

One good coat passes through four owners in a year (sold, lost, given, won at cards), and each owner alters it a little.

Possible heart: things carry the people who wore them.

- **Uses:** [Clothing and Material Culture](../../Culture/Clothing-and-Material-Culture.md): repair, reuse and how fashion travels.
- **Needs:** keep the phrase "traveling coat" out; the checker reserves it for a link between stories. As a [Thread](../Notes/The-Long-Road.md#kinds-of-story) story, a later owner could be someone Wurdren knows.

#### How the News Came

`DOMINO` · *a relay post on a pass road · Season 2*

A big piece of news reaches a remote relay post in three versions, by three carriers, in one day. The postkeeper has to decide which one goes on the board.

Possible heart: getting it right slowly beats getting it wrong first.

Possible thread: what people say about the big events gets "more specific, and sometimes more wrong" ([The Long Road](../Notes/The-Long-Road.md#what-the-reader-can-piece-together)).

- **Uses:** [rumor, verification and correction](../../Movement-and-Information.md#8-rumor-verification-and-correction), [scheduled carriage](../../Couriers-Posts-and-Message-Networks.md#scheduled-carriage).

### Picks

Not a ranking. Each is picked for a different reason, so a season could start from any of them.

| Pick | Why |

### Plan

How the stories are made doesn't change. Each picked seed runs the [pipeline](../Notes/Pipeline.md) from Stage 0, and a batch starts with a test story.

1. **bt 7274 picks** which seeds go forward, and answers the [questions](#questions-for-bt-7274) for any seed that needs them.
2. **Test story first:** lean, Mending Day, because it tests the new thing (a story with no thread) and needs no new lore. Its cold read checks one extra question: does it feel like part of the same book?
3. **Season 2's mix:** lean, about half World and half Domino, with Wurdren passing through one or two of the Domino stories, and the rung from [The Long Road](../Notes/The-Long-Road.md#the-seasons-loosely).
4. **After drafting,** rerun the [coverage](../../../atlas/coverage/Coverage.md) report to see what the season added.

### Questions for bt 7274

One at a time, each with a lean.

1. **Seven Hill: where is it, and whose base was it?** Lean: on a Spine pass, a fortress emptied when Highridge's toll wars gave way to negotiated passage, so it has been a town for generations and the base is the town's old bones. The other way would be a recent garrison stood down by a treaty, which makes it fresher and sorer.
2. **The Overpath: who walks it?** Lean: a chain of old herders' and pilgrims' paths that came to be walked as one, walked by pilgrims, herders, couriers who know a short cut, and people with reasons of their own. Nobody runs it.
3. **The circuit people:** does the Appalachian-influenced mobile people have a name for itself? Lean: not yet. The stories can call them by their family names, the way they'd call themselves.
4. **The Warrior's, Assassin's and Spy Guilds:** what are they, if anything, before a story uses them? Lean: park The Tea Boy and The Retired Blade until then.
