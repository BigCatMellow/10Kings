# The Greenvale Man

## Status

**Provisional story concept.** Written with the [Sunday Morning framework](../Framework.md). Kettle Cove, Narrow Sound, all characters and plot details are new and non-canon. The regatta is a story detail attached to the provisional Last Sail festival, not established custom.

**Development level: L3 Drafted** — [read the draft](../Drafts/The-Greenvale-Man.md) (fourth pass, checked for fit and uniqueness across the collection; not yet reviewed). Outlined at L2; developed through the [Story Pipeline](../Story-Pipeline.md). The L0 concept is preserved in git history (commit `3427bdd`).

## Premise

Kettle Cove's racing boat cracks a hull plank weeks before the Last Sail regatta against the rival cove. The boatbuilder has retired inland to live with his son in Greenvale. The only woodworker left is a Greenvale-born cooper who has lived in the cove for twenty years and is still called "the Greenvale man."

**Setting:** Kettle Cove, a small [Northwind](../../Regions/Northwind.md) fishing town (provisional name)
**Clock:** [Last Sail](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#seasonal-calendar), late autumn — about three weeks
**Mode:** competition / belonging

## Protagonist

**Aldo Fenwright** — cooper.

- **Good at:** steam-bending staves, tight joins, and fixing anything that has to hold water.
- **Small flaw:** seasick, and far too polite about being treated as a guest after twenty years.
- **Why he resists:** "I make barrels." Also, he gets seasick.

## Place

A cove town built around a sheltered harbor, drying racks and inland gardens. Everyone is related to someone in Narrow Sound across the headland. It has been a bad year for raids up the coast, and Maris Bleakshore has called on every cove to choose convoy partners it can trust.

Recurring locations:

1. **The boat shed** — cold, crowded, full of opinions.
2. **The cooperage** — warm, quiet, Aldo's territory, with a steam box.
3. **The drying racks** — where gossip moves at the speed of fish-turning.
4. **The bell tower** — where the regatta bell should hang; it has hung in Narrow Sound for three years.
5. **The clan hall** — where decisions are made and remembered.
6. **The slipway** — where nobody has yet seen the repaired boat float.

## Cast

- **Hild Fenwright** — Aldo's wife, a Northwind net-mender who finds all of this extremely funny.
- **Brenna Scarth** — the old boatbuilder's daughter. Can sail anything; can't build.
- **Old Rask** — a clan elder who pointedly uses the exclusive "we" around Aldo.
- **The rival cove's champion rower** — everyone's cousin. Her uncle lost a boat to raiders this summer, so the rumor that Narrow Sound shelters them lands on her family like an insult at a funeral.
- **The old boatbuilder** — present only through letters from Harveston Vale, where he lives with his son, complaining that the country is too flat. His latest describes a town that held two festivals at once and ate everything.

## Problem

Without a sound boat, Kettle Cove must forfeit the regatta or borrow a boat from Narrow Sound. Borrowing would be discussed until the next century.

## Complications

1. A cracked hull is not a barrel, though steaming and fitting a plank uses the same skills as steaming a stave.
2. Old Rask objects to an outsider working on the cove's boat.
3. Good seasoned planking is short at the end of the season.
4. Narrow Sound's children spy on the shed.
5. Storms push back the test launch. For weeks nobody has *seen* the boat float, so in Northwind grammar its seaworthiness can only be reported as rumor.
6. *It is said* that Narrow Sound shelters raiders. Half the clan hall wants the regatta called off and Narrow Sound left out of any convoy.

## Running elements

- Inclusive versus exclusive "we" ([Northwind languages](../../Culture/Language-and-Thought.md#northwind-languages)).
- The boat's seaworthiness, moving from *it is said* to *I saw it*.
- The old builder's letters about how flat Greenvale is.
- "Soft as Greenvale butter" — said to Aldo once, early, and disproved without comment. See [Regional Social Dynamics](../../Culture/Regional-Social-Dynamics.md#example-sayings).
- [Frosted Mead Cakes](../../Culture/Recipes/Northwind.md#4-frosted-mead-cakes) as race-day food.

## Emotional core

Belonging can be built. Aldo has belonged for twenty years; the cove simply hasn't had a reason to say so, and in Northwind speech, saying so needs a witness.

## Climax

Someone has to ride the repaired boat on its test launch and bail if the new plank weeps. Aldo goes, seasick the whole way, bailing steadily while it takes up. Old Rask watches from the slipway. It floats. That night the clan hall votes on calling off the regatta over the Narrow Sound rumor. Rask asks who saw it. Nobody did. "I have seen the Greenvale man bail a boat he mended," he says. "I have not seen Narrow Sound shelter anyone." The regatta goes ahead. Kettle Cove races hard and loses narrowly.

## Soft landing

On the walk home Rask tells the story of the test launch tagged *I saw it*, then says, "We'll take them next year" — and this time it is the "we" that includes Aldo. The bell stays across the headland. A last letter arrives from Greenvale: the builder asks whether the plank held. Before the race, the champion rower thanks Rask, briefly and without saying for what; three other coves have already left Narrow Sound out of their convoys. The Long Dark Feast is coming. Hild opens the first winter sack of Greenvale flour, stamped with a town square's two names, and Aldo laughs at something he doesn't explain. Somebody wants a new barrel.

### Stage 1 — THINK

**Reasoning allocation:** structured single path. The premise asks a cooper to do boatwork, a craft-plausibility claim a reader could catch out, and the emotional payoff hinges on a grammar feature.

**Decomposition.** Separable problems:

1. what is wrong with the boat;
2. why Aldo can fix it;
3. the town's objection;
4. the race and its outcome;
5. how belonging becomes visible;
6. the clock.

Connection to keep: the moment that earns the inclusive "we" must be something Rask personally witnesses, or the payoff contradicts the evidential grammar.

**Assumption mapping**

| Assumption | Status | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A cooper can build a racing boat in weeks | `ASSUMED` → **false** | Too big a leap. Replaced by repairing one cracked hull plank with Brenna's knowledge, which a cooper's steaming and fitting skills plausibly cover. |
| Northwind has local timber | `VERIFIED` | [Northwind](../../Regions/Northwind.md#geography): forests in milder zones; late-season shortage is plausible. |
| Last Sail falls in late autumn | `VERIFIED` (provisional festival) | [Seasonal calendar](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#seasonal-calendar). |
| Inclusive/exclusive "we" and evidentials | `VERIFIED` as design direction | [Language and Thought](../../Culture/Language-and-Thought.md#northwind-languages). |
| Losing the race still satisfies | `ASSUMED` → **survives** | The payoff is recognition, not the bell. |

**First principles.** Belonging here must be recognized, not granted by winning. In Northwind speech recognition needs a witness, so the elder's change must come from something he *saw*. That turns the evidential running gag into the mechanism: rumor → witnessed → inclusive "we."

**Counterexample search**

| Attack | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Why doesn't a Narrow Sound builder fix it? | **Survives.** Asking the rival cove is the same humiliation as borrowing their boat. |
| Aldo rows in the race and wins it for them | **Contradicts** the character (seasick) and the theme. He rides only the test launch. |
| Rask's change of heart is sudden | **Narrows.** He brings his saved planking in s6, still using the exclusive "we." |

**PLAN handoff**

```text
frame             a cove's outsider-insider earns the inclusive "we" by being seen to bail a boat he repaired
selected strategy one cracked plank, steamed like a stave; seasick test launch; lose narrowly
alternatives      A build a new boat — set aside: implausible in weeks
                  B win the regatta — set aside: shifts payoff from recognition to victory
assumptions       regatta is a provisional detail on the Last Sail festival
unknowns          Kettle Cove's clan structure stays vague
reconsider if     the plank repair needs technical exposition; the elder's turn feels unearned
```
