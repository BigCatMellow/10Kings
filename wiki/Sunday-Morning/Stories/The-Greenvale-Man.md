# The Greenvale Man

## Status

**Provisional story concept.** Written with the [Sunday Morning framework](../Framework.md). Kettle Cove, Narrow Sound, all characters and plot details are new and non-canon. The regatta is a story detail attached to the provisional Last Sail festival, not established custom.

## Premise

Kettle Cove's racing boat cracks weeks before the Last Sail regatta against the rival cove. The boatbuilder has retired inland to live with his son in Greenvale. The only woodworker left is a Greenvale-born cooper who has lived in the cove for twenty years and is still called "the Greenvale man."

**Setting:** Kettle Cove, a small [Northwind](../../Regions/Northwind.md) fishing town (provisional name)
**Clock:** [Last Sail](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#seasonal-calendar), late autumn
**Mode:** competition / belonging

## Protagonist

**Aldo Fenwright** — cooper.

- **Good at:** steam-bending staves, tight joins, and fixing anything that holds liquid.
- **Small flaw:** gets seasick, and is far too polite about being treated as a guest after twenty years.
- **Why he resists:** "I make barrels." Also, he gets seasick.

## Place

A cove town built around a sheltered harbor, drying racks and inland gardens. Everyone is related to someone in the rival cove across the headland.

Recurring locations:

1. **The boat shed** — cold, crowded, full of opinions.
2. **The cooperage** — warm, quiet, Aldo's territory.
3. **The drying racks** — where gossip happens at the speed of fish-turning.
4. **The bell tower** — home of the regatta bell, currently held by Narrow Sound.
5. **The clan hall** — where decisions are made and remembered.
6. **The slipway** — where nobody has yet seen the new boat float.

## Cast

- **Aldo's wife** — a Northwind net-mender who finds all of this extremely funny.
- **The boatbuilder's daughter** — can sail anything; can't build.
- **A clan elder** — pointedly uses the exclusive "we" around Aldo.
- **The rival cove's champion rower** — everyone's cousin.
- **The old boatbuilder** — present only through letters from Greenvale complaining that the country is too flat.

## Problem

Without a boat, Kettle Cove forfeits the regatta and Narrow Sound keeps the bell another year. Worse, the cove would have to *borrow* a boat — from Narrow Sound.

## Complications

1. Barrels are not boats — though steam-bending staves turns out to be a shared technique.
2. The clan elder objects to an outsider building the cove's boat.
3. Good seasoned planking is short at the end of the season.
4. Narrow Sound's kids spy on the shed.
5. Storms push back the test launch. For weeks nobody has *seen* the boat float, so in Northwind grammar its seaworthiness can only be stated as rumor.

## Running elements

- Inclusive versus exclusive "we" ([Northwind languages](../../Culture/Language-and-Thought.md#northwind-languages)).
- Evidential statements about the boat: seen, told, rumored.
- The old builder's letters about how flat Greenvale is.
- "Soft as Greenvale butter" — said to Aldo once, early, and disproved without comment. See [Regional Social Dynamics](../../Culture/Regional-Social-Dynamics.md).
- [Frosted Mead Cakes](../../Culture/Recipes/Northwind.md#4-frosted-mead-cakes) as race-day food.

## Emotional core

Belonging can be built. Aldo has been part of the town for twenty years; the town simply hasn't had a reason to say so.

## Climax

The whole cove helps finish the boat: the daughter rigs it, the net-menders caulk it, the clan elder brings the planking he'd been saving for his own repairs. It floats. Kettle Cove races and loses narrowly.

## Soft landing

On the walk home the elder says, "We'll take them next year" — and this time it is the "we" that includes Aldo. The bell stays across the headland. The Long Dark Feast is coming, and somebody wants a new barrel for it.
