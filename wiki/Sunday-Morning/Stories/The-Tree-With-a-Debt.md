# The Tree With a Debt

## Status

**Provisional story concept.** Written with the [Sunday Morning framework](../Framework.md). All characters, the tree, the lien and plot details are new and non-canon.

## Premise

A Deepwood forest warden and a Highridge route surveyor are ordered to route the new mule path to Twilighthollow's herb market around an ancient tree. Then they discover the town put the tree up as collateral on a Highridge loan sixty years ago and never paid it back.

**Setting:** [Twilighthollow](../../Places/Border-Towns.md#twilighthollow--deepwood--highridge) (Deepwood / Highridge)
**Clock:** [Canopy Vigil and Midsummer Debates](../../Culture/Festivals-and-Seasonal-Life.md#seasonal-calendar), midsummer, with the herb fair right after
**Mode:** romance

## Protagonists

**Sessa Alderwater** — forest warden.

- **Good at:** reading ground, water and animal sign; knowing what a place is doing.
- **Small flaw:** treats every outsider's question as a threat until proven otherwise.
- **Why she resists:** a path near that tree is exactly the kind of thing wardens exist to stop.

**Ismet Carrow** — Highridge route surveyor.

- **Good at:** measurement, obligations, and precisely what a promise commits someone to.
- **Small flaw:** footnotes his own speech.
- **Why he resists:** he was promised a simple job, and he is allergic to anything with a moral dimension that isn't in writing.

## Place

A town where forest uplands meet exposed plateau routes. Mixed families of rangers, traders, herders and guides; the Deepwood half of town and the Highridge half of town keep different midsummer customs on the same days.

Recurring locations:

1. **The tree** — older than the town; carved with marks from several eras.
2. **The herb market** — the reason the path matters.
3. **The vigil grounds** — Deepwood side, deliberately quiet.
4. **The debate hall** — Highridge side, deliberately not.
5. **The mule yard** — home of Contract.
6. **The records office** — including a lien register nobody has opened in decades.

## Cast

- **Contract** — a mule who refuses the surveyed line every time.
- **The market master** — needs the path open before the herb fair.
- **The lender's descendant** — arrives from the plateau expecting either payment or a tree.
- **A Weaver-path elder** — documenting everything, including the romance, in the community record. See [Path of the Great Weaver](../../Politics/Religions.md#2-path-of-the-great-weaver).
- **A kid who climbs the tree** — the only person in town who talks to it directly.

## Problem

In Sessa's language the tree belongs to the grammatical class of living participants. In Ismet's records it is "Lot 14, encumbered." The path can't be built until they agree what the tree is, and the herb fair won't wait.

## Complications

1. Canopy Vigil halts all work: they are obliged to sit together and simply observe the tree for a day, while the Highridge half of town debates at full volume across the square.
2. They disagree on what the tree *is*, then on what a path *is*, then on what "around" means.
3. The lender's descendant arrives.
4. The herb fair deadline moves closer.
5. Contract still refuses the surveyed line.

## Running elements

- Contract.
- The debates audible over the vigil.
- Ismet's footnotes.
- Sessa's several words for "old," none of which translate.
- The Weaver elder writing things down at the worst moments.

## Emotional core

Two professional vocabularies turn out to describe the same care. This is the border speech the wiki describes — "environmental categories with contractual precision" — becoming a relationship. It also touches Deepwood's larger question, at the smallest possible scale: [who gets to define stewardship](../../Regions/Deepwood.md#story-function).

## Climax

The town pays off the lien together, each household contributing a coin, the Weaver elder recording every name. The path follows Contract's preferred line, which turns out to trace the edge of the root zone Sessa was protecting. Sessa and Ismet co-sign the survey in a hybrid register neither of their guilds would accept alone.

## Soft landing

The tree ends up with a name in both languages and a clean ledger. The herb fair opens on time. The lender's descendant decides to stay and open a tea stall by the new path, which is the next small problem.
