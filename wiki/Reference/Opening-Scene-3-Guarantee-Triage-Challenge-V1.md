- a powerful commercial network can respond faster than ordinary politics;

> **the harmed transaction is sitting too neatly beside the helped transaction.**

That makes hidden triage feel staged for the reader.

---

# 1. Keep Wurdren's success scene focused

At the guarantor office, Wurdren / the Grain Agent need only understand:

- the substitute warehouse is recognized;
- supplemental backing is available for this staple transfer;
- the backing ends at settlement;
- the fee is unpleasant but survivable.

Do not have them receive a mini-lecture on:

- finite exposure;
- who else loses;
- portfolio allocation.

The office can say only enough to justify its decision.

Strong working phrasing:

> **"Staple transfers caught by recognized warehouse substitutions are being covered today if the underlying sale is otherwise clean."**

That sounds like:

- temporary commercial policy;
- not secret strategy.

---

# 2. Move the cost to a Records Worker handoff

After Wurdren's POV closes, use a **very short Records Worker continuation**.

Purpose:

- show the cost exists;
- preserve documentary residue;
- avoid coincidence;
- establish that ordinary clerks can later notice patterns.

The worker receives:

1. Wurdren / Grain Agent's completed transfer:
   - supplemental backing;
   - recognized warehouse;
   - settlement closed;

2. an unrelated small transaction:
   - extension shortened;
   - guarantee declined;
   - or covered amount reduced.

The two papers contain:

- the same correspondent house / network mark;
- or the same temporary policy wording.

The worker does not infer a Council.

They infer only:

> **somebody with broader authority is reallocating risk today.**

That is enough.

---

# 3. Why this is stronger

## It preserves reader-knowledge discipline

The reader sees:

- one good outcome;
- one bad outcome.

But not:

- the governing logic of the whole system;
- who coordinated it;
- why the correspondent network chose this priority.

## It makes the Records Worker useful later

The worker now has a plausible memory:

> same mark / same phrase appeared on several unrelated adjustments.

That can later become one fragment in the Council evidence mosaic.

## It avoids moral simplification

The harmed transaction should not obviously be:

- frivolous;
- luxury;
- villainous.

It should be an ordinary small business / borrower whose claim simply loses priority.

That keeps the problem:

> coordination requires allocation,

not:

> good food versus bad luxury.

---

# 4. Information-budget correction

V1 currently lets the guarantor clerk explain:

> the office has limited exposure and is backing more staple cargo.

That is plausible but slightly too much system interpretation in one exchange.

V2 should narrow the explanation to:

- a temporary staple-transfer authority exists;
- their case qualifies;
- approval is finite / conditional only insofar as the terms say so.

The reader can infer scarcity from the later Records Worker handoff.
