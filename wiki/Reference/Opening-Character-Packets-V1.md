# 1. Wurdren — opening-state packet

## What he says he wants

- work;
- travel;
- a change;
- something less dull than staying put.

## What he actually wants

> **an accomplishment large enough to prove that his life has not been wasted.**

He is not consciously lying about wanting adventure.

Adventure is the form his deeper need has taken.

## What he is good at

Not spectacularly:

- swordsmanship;
- tactics;
- scholarship;
- politics.

Reliably:

- reading a tense room;
- escort discipline;
- noticing when a story has a missing piece;
- remembering practical details;
- keeping frightened people moving;
- staying when leaving would be easier;
- talking to people who do not usually get treated as important;
- recognizing when two people are arguing about different things.

## Opening blind spot

He undervalues exactly the competencies that make him useful because they do not feel legendary.

He thinks:

> competence in ordinary trouble is what you do between adventures.

The story will gradually reverse that hierarchy.

## Age cues

Do not establish exact age yet.

Show age through:

- stiffness after long travel;
- knowing how to conserve effort;
- old habits younger workers have not learned;
- people younger than him assuming he needs help;
- seeing Port features that used to be different;
- remembering obsolete guild / route practices.

Avoid:

- constant aching;
- frailty shorthand;
- "too old for this" jokes every scene.

## Opening emotional contradiction

He is:

- proud enough to want significance;
- decent enough to keep doing small obligations anyway.

That contradiction is the engine.

# 2. Port records / contract worker

## Working placeholder

**The Records Worker**

Do not name yet.

## Institutional position

Permanent or long-term Port records professional working within one of the newer **shared clerical / reciprocal-recognition arrangements** used by several guild / commercial networks.

During Three Moons, they are temporarily assigned heavily to:

- warehouse transfers;
- credential checks;
- copied contracts;
- guarantor / buyer mismatches.

They are not:

- a judge;
- a Council member;
- a secret investigator.

## Broad origin / naming constraint

Working default:

> **Port-born, from a household with at least one generation of migration / trade connection outside Port.**

This is enough to justify:

- strong Port identity;
- familiarity with several naming / record forms;
- possible difference between household name and standardized legal spelling.

Do not select source region yet.

## Immediate wants

- clear the queue correctly;
- avoid inheriting liability for somebody else's shortcut;
- finish enough work to preserve professional reputation;
- get through Three Moons without the shared registry becoming an excuse for large houses to dump impossible work on clerks.

## Independent life

Choose one small off-camera obligation during drafting.

Strong candidate:

> they have promised to meet a sibling / household member after shift and are already late.

The relationship need not enter the main plot.

Its purpose is to prove the worker has a life outside Wurdren.

## Political tendency

Supports:

- shared records;
- reciprocal recognition;
- practical coordination.

Skeptical of:

- large houses pressuring clerks;
- permanent concentration of authority;
- the assumption that faster means more legitimate.

They do not need a developed constitutional ideology yet.

## Contradiction

They believe rules protect ordinary people from powerful actors.

They also know powerful actors are better at using the rules.

## Speech / behavior cues

- asks for names, not "the man at the warehouse";
- distinguishes "accepted," "stored," "certified," and "guaranteed";
- dislikes people saying two documents are "basically the same";
- dry rather than theatrical;
- becomes more cooperative when Wurdren stops trying to simplify the problem.

## Wurdren relationship

Starts as:

> tired clerk + another traveler who thinks urgency should move him to the front.

Changes because Wurdren:

- listens;
- retrieves what is missing;
- does not blame the worker for the system;
- remembers their name / instructions later.

The worker remembers him because he was **less difficult than expected**, not because destiny sparked.

## Later function

- recognizes repeated guarantors / procedures;
- preserves ordinary records;
- becomes one fragment in the Council evidence mosaic.

# 3. Port labor / neighborhood contact

## Working placeholder

**The Old Port Hand**

Do not name yet.

## Relationship to Wurdren

Working default:

> **a former road / contract-work acquaintance who traveled with Wurdren for several seasons years ago, then deliberately stopped taking long-distance work and settled in Port.**

They were close enough to:

- know Wurdren before his current regret hardened;
- joke without ceremony;
- notice he is "back out" for reasons he is not saying.

They do not need to have been his closest friend.

## Current occupation

Working class:

> **coordinates or supervises working crews around the river-quay / warehouse / roadhead interface.**

Exact guild / employment status remains open.

This gives them practical knowledge of:

- labor demand;
- storage;
- unloading;
- festival overtime;
- broken equipment;
- outsider expectations.

## Broad origin / naming constraint

They may be:

- Port-born;
- or a long-settled migrant who now identifies primarily with Port.

Do not choose until naming work needs it.

Their identity should support:

> Port is a real home, not merely a neutral workplace.

## Immediate wants

During Three Moons:

- keep crews paid;
- keep inexperienced temporary workers from getting hurt;
- keep unloading schedules from collapsing;
- get one delayed piece of repair work completed.

Longer-term:

- Port retaining enough revenue / authority to maintain the infrastructure everyone uses.

## Political tendency

Supports stronger Port capacity.

Distrusts:

- outside powers treating Port as shared equipment;
- large guild / merchant offices claiming that Port autonomy means their autonomy;
- centralization that shifts cost onto labor / neighborhoods.

This lets them agree with the Villain's grievance without becoming his follower.

## Contradiction

They chose ordinary rooted life and genuinely value it.

They still sometimes envy the road.

Do not make them a smug "settled person who knows better."

## Speech / behavior cues

- practical;
- mildly profane if setting voice supports it;
- jokes about historic events becoming overtime;
- knows which complaints are new and which recur every festival;
- asks Wurdren concrete questions instead of emotional ones.

## Complete-person function

This character does **not** tell Wurdren:

> your ordinary acts already make your life meaningful.

Their existence quietly demonstrates:

> a life can become dense with responsibility, relationship, skill, and belonging without becoming legendary.

Wurdren respects that and still initially excludes it from the standard he applies to himself.

## Later function

- Port below-the-elite perspective;
- human route back into wartime strain;
- settlement test: does greater Port agency materially help people who live there?

# 4. Longwood recurring participant

## Working placeholder

**The Corridor Delegate**

Do not name yet.

## Institutional / political location

Working default:

> **from the western-edge Longwood political concentration (LW-W class), where town / compact authority overlaps with older local use rights and outside route / extraction pressure.**

They are not:

- ruler of Longwood;
- voice of all forest people;
- mystical warden speaking for nature.

Their mandate is limited.

## Role

Strong current option:

> **community-recognized rights recorder / delegate with practical knowledge of corridor agreements and local use claims.**

They came to Port because:

- the route / infrastructure party has Port guild / commercial representation;
- a copied concession / annex is being used in contract discussions there;
- correcting the record before work expands is cheaper than fighting after clearing begins.

## Immediate want

Not:

> stop all roads.

They want:

- passage agreement honored as written;
- cutting / widening rights not smuggled into a passage grant;
- local parties involved before physical work changes the corridor;
- the dispute sent back with accurate records and a process capable of reaching a local settlement.

## Contradiction

They are more open to outside trade / negotiated access than some people at home.

That makes them:

- useful to reform;
- vulnerable to accusations of giving too much away.

They can sincerely believe both:

> the route matters

and:

> outsiders do not get to redefine what was granted.

## Speech / behavior cues

- precise about verbs;
- corrects "ownership" into specific rights;
- asks "right to do what?";
- dislikes people treating all forest authority as one thing;
- can become sharp when a Port official says "the local owners" as though one owner exists.

## First impression of Wurdren

Likely negative / cautious.

He looks like:

- hired muscle;
- another traveler attached to commercial people;
- someone who will want the problem simplified.

Trust begins when he asks:

> which right are they actually claiming?

rather than:

> who owns the land?

## Relationship with Wurdren

The relationship should grow through:

- shared travel;
- practical disagreement;
- seeing him admit when he does not know local law;
- seeing him keep a promise after the Port scene ends.

They do not become his moral teacher.

## Later function

- helps produce genuine Longwood counter-domino;
- provides later precedent for bounded emergency authority;
- becomes one person in the cross-boundary trust network.

# 5. Opening grain merchant / carrier

## Working placeholder

**The Grain Agent**

## Status

**Limited-role by default.**

Do not promote yet.

## Function

- gives Wurdren a concrete job;
- humanizes Low Rivers abundance / debt stress;
- makes warehouse / guarantee failure matter.

## Need

Keep them specific enough to care about:

- payment;
- storage;
- next contract;
- people expecting money back home.

But do not automatically give them a late-story role.

## Promotion condition

Promote only if the post-festival Low Rivers stabilization is stronger when the same person experiences:

- the successful intervention;
- the later debt / ownership afterlife.

Otherwise let them remain one person Wurdren helped.

# 6. Relationship triangle in the opening

The three recurring opening relationships should pressure different parts of Wurdren.

## Records Worker

Challenges:

> **what counts as important work?**

Their invisible precision saves people from real loss.

## Old Port Hand

Challenges:

> **what counts as an accomplished life?**

Their rooted ordinary life is dense with meaning Wurdren can see but not yet apply to himself.

## Corridor Delegate

Challenges:

> **what counts as useful heroism?**

The problem is solved through attention, specificity, patience, and relationship rather than force.

This is enough.

Do not add a fourth "philosophical foil" in the opening.

# 7. Naming decision

**Do not name these characters yet.**

That is deliberate, not avoidance.

Per [Personal Naming Framework](Personal-Naming-Framework.md), final names should follow:

- birthplace / naming community;
- language history;
- legal / social naming form;
- migration;
- generation;
- class / occupation.

The scene architecture does not yet require final names.

The next naming pass should be **character-specific**, not a general list:

1. establish the Port records worker's family / language history;
2. establish the Old Port Hand's origin / settlement history;
3. establish the Corridor Delegate's specific LW-W naming community;
4. generate small candidate sets;
5. stress-test them against existing naming history.

Until then, drafting may use role placeholders.
