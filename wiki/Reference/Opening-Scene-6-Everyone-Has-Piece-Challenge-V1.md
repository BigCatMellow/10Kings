# 1. Remove the final systems inventory

V1 ends by explicitly grouping:

- sailors / convoy;
- grain;
- animals;
- pins;
- Wurdren's papers.

That is too close to the author-side pressure board.

Better ending:

- Wurdren thinks about:
  - tomorrow's departure;
  - finding a bed;
  - whether the lanterns were actually supposed to match;
  - perhaps the fact that every person in Port seems convinced their own delay is the urgent one.

Do **not** ask him to compare the fragments yet.

The reader can remember them.

is enough.

# 4. Stone Hills handoff

Scene 6:

The lantern beat should remain because it prevents every movement through Port from becoming a clue-delivery system.

If trimming later, cut repeated transition prose before cutting the plotless household detail.
