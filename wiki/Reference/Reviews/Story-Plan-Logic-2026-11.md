## Result

**The plan works as a structure.**
- The rules are consistent with each other.
- The season mix holds (seven Domino and seven World a season, two expositions).
- Wurdren's appearances stay within his limit of two or three a season.
- The clue ration is coherent, the cross-story threads have hosts, and every season has a turn story.
- Season 1 is internally sound.

**Its logic has three gaps and one outright contradiction:**
1. **A contradiction, now fixed.** Three pages and two map data files had the Villain "amplifying Council involvement". Under D28 he doesn't know the Council exists.
2. **How a "regular dude" reaches six regions** is never said: the money, the people, the network.
3. **Season 2's "more than one hand"** has no carrier the reader can recognize as a second hand.
4. **The war's border is unchosen,** and the threads point two ways.

There are also smaller strains, listed below. **What bt 7274 needs to decide is in *Questions*, most important first.**
