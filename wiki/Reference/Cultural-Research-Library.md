# Cultural Research Library — Two Sons / 10Kings

## Status and use

**Non-canon research/reference. Updated 2026-11-04.** This is the entry point to cultural research relevant to the setting, **not** a second canon or a replacement for the 220 preserved source notes. [Current cultural owner](../Culture/Culture-and-Influence.md) and [World Rules](../World-Rules.md) outrank this index. [Source Register](Source-Register.md) records ingestion/disposition.

This archive distinguishes four kinds of material:
1. **PRESERVED:** Original older notes kept in the repository; the original text is authoritative evidence of what was brainstormed, **not** authoritative setting canon.
2. **RECOVERED SUMMARY:** Specific older chat subjects recovered through conversation history, but not a verified full transcript. Historical wording/date/details may be incomplete.
3. **EXTERNALLY VERIFIED:** Reference material checked against named institutional or scholarly sources on 2026-11-04; sources are linked at point of use.
4. **OPEN:** Names, candidate analogues, or topics awaiting primary sources, community perspectives and correction.

**Rule:** A real community is not a costume, personality type, moral stereotype, or single-trait template. Study **mechanisms** (rights, labor, ecology, exchange, performance, memory, language, law, technology), along with who participates, who benefits, variations, and historical change.

## Especially valuable cross-disciplinary mechanisms

| Mechanism to investigate | Real-world case in [practice research](Cultural-Practices-and-Institutions.md) | Two Sons world question, **not a proposed answer** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice as instrument and social structure | Sardinian canto a tenore, Georgian polyphony, Inuit katajjaq (historical lead) | Who sings? At work, mourning, celebrations, contest, private home? What is shared across class lines? |
| Language carried through landscape | Silbo Gomero | Which valley or maritime network values distance signaling, and who teaches it? |
| Material records beyond standard alphabetic writing | Inka khipu, Vanuatu sand drawings | Who can authenticate a record? Who owns expertise? Which material fails in fire or water? |
| Ritual governance of resources | Balinese subak | Who maintains irrigation, schedules distribution, resolves upstream–downstream conflict? |
| Prestige through reciprocal exchange | Trobriand Kula network | How does reciprocal obligation differ from paying for ordinary goods? |
| Recitation and trained expertise | Yoruba Ifá | How do specialists transmit large corpora, qualify apprentices and disagree on interpretation? |
| Performance as public memory and commentary | Indonesian wayang, Garifuna oral song traditions | Can satire, narrative and history spread across markets without an official archive? |
| Land, identity and infrastructure | Bedu of Petra/Wadi Rum, Ainu ritual and dance | How do environmental knowledge, hospitality and sacred practice overlap without becoming generic 'nature magic'? |
| Differing social institutions within the same geography | Regional contact-zone research | What changes first: language, tools, family roles, cuisine, law, housing or identity? |

## Reconciliation principles

1. Study the real practice on its own terms; record at least one named source and avoid treating all members of a community alike.
2. Extract the **problem and social mechanism**, not an aesthetic composite.
3. Compare the mechanism with current Two Sons ecology, institutions and technology. Require plausible material costs, labor, routes and historical transmission.
4. Leave a plausible **contact history**: intermarriage, migration, apprentices, pilgrims, merchants, class, forced displacement, shared rights, political friction, generational change.
5. If a design changes established setting facts, reconcile it with the owning page first. Otherwise keep as a question or provisional candidate.
6. Consider religious and sacred elements separately. Their existence is not automatic permission to reproduce restricted knowledge or ritual.
7. When making a fictional example, identify it explicitly as **hypothetical**.

## Remaining coverage gaps

- Full transcripts are still unavailable for multiple 2024–2026 conversations; only partial summaries and the preserved source corpus can be indexed.
- The older real-world cultural research rarely records **which specific community provided which mechanism to which local Two Sons community**; previous reviews already identified this as a gap.
- Community-level cultural descriptions need more than cross-region 'inspiration pools': daily economy, governance, gender/age/class differences, internal arguments, changes over time, and neighbors' perceptions.
- Additional firsthand, community-produced, and peer-reviewed sources are needed for disputed or sacred traditions. The existence of a UNESCO page is evidence of the described tradition, not blanket validation of every downstream interpretation.
