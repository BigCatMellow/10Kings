## Summary

**Character / city structure passes. Theme delivery is slightly too explicit.**

The scene already succeeds at:

- making Port feel inhabited;
- giving Wurdren a genuine old relationship;
- showing the Old Port Hand's independent obligations;
- establishing support for stronger Port capacity without Port unanimity;
- seeding Netman without exposition;
- handing naturally into Longwood.

The bounded problem:

> **the Old Port Hand occasionally speaks like the story's thematic summary rather than like someone describing their own life.**

---

# 1. Main line to cut back

V1:

> "I got tired of leaving every decent thing because I thought the next road would prove something this one hadn't."

This is almost exactly Wurdren's arc stated by someone else.

Better:

> **"I got tired of packing every time a place started to feel ordinary."**

Why stronger:

- autobiographical;
- concrete;
- slightly self-critical;
- Wurdren can make the comparison himself.

The Old Port Hand does not need to understand Wurdren's whole problem.

---

# 2. Political line to de-thesis

V1:

> "I argue with people who think useful means they own the future."

This is too close to the story-level thesis.

Replace with something tied to their actual work.

Example class:

> **"Useful is fine. I just don't want every quay in Port waiting on one office because the office decided it knew better."**

or:

> **"Useful isn't the same as I want them running my crew."**

That preserves:

- support for coordination;
- distrust of authority concentration

without putting the novel's conclusion in Scene 4.

---

# 3. Old Port Hand voice

The shared-history banter is plausible.

Still, preserve a distinction:

## Wurdren

- deflects;
- turns discomfort into jokes;
- keeps conversation slightly sideways.

## Old Port Hand

- answers more directly;
- jokes through work / familiarity;
- does not decorate explanations.

Their best lines are things like:

> "I work here."

and:

> "Depends which Port you ask."

Those feel grounded.

---

# 4. The life itself should carry the argument

Keep:

- broken lift;
- wrong timber street;
- niece expecting them home;
- cookshop familiarity;
- keys / responsibility;
- people asking them things.

These details do more thematic work than any explicit statement.

The reader should be able to conclude:

> this life is dense with meaning

before Wurdren can.
