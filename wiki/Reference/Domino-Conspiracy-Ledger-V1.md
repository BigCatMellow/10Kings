The war chain describes **what conditions change**.

This page describes the kind of small hidden work required to make selected changes more likely.

The reader should not see this as a checklist.

Many items happen:

- off-page;
- through intermediaries;
- for ordinary commercial reasons;
- without the person performing the act knowing the larger objective.

---

# 1. Execution rule

A large conspiracy should require:

- money;
- clerks;
- brokers;
- couriers;
- buyers;
- guides;
- warehouse space;
- trusted contacts;
- copies of documents;
- timing;
- mistakes;
- compartmentation.

It should **not** require hundreds of people to know:

> "we are starting a war."

Most participants know only the task they were paid / persuaded to perform.

# 12. The conspiracy's scale

V1 therefore already supports roughly:

- **40 major causal state changes** in the war chain;
- **79 possible micro-actions / support tasks** in this execution ledger;
- many more ordinary responses that are not conspiratorial at all.

Do **not** treat 79 as a quota.

The value is demonstrating that a continental conspiracy can be deep and labor-intensive without requiring 79 spectacular crimes.

Most are:

- information;
- timing;
- purchases;
- copying;
- routing;
- introductions;
- small financing;
- observation.

That is what lets the conspiracy exist in the background of ordinary life.

# 13. Compartmentation

A useful structure is:

## Inner strategic circle

Knows:

- Port objective;
- war forcing mechanism;
- several major leverage points.

Very small.

## Operational coordinators

Know:

- several connected tasks;
- that they are politically motivated;
- not necessarily the entire war chain.

## Specialists

Know:

- one craft;
- one client;
- one immediate objective.

Examples:

- broker;
- forger;
- carrier;
- lender;
- clerk;
- security contractor.

## Useful allies

Know:

- the movement helps their grievance;
- little or nothing about covert manipulation.

## Unwitting actors

Simply make normal decisions inside conditions the conspiracy helped shape.

This is the largest category.

# 14. Ah-ha audit

When the conspiracy is later investigated, the evidence should reveal **layers**, not one confession.

Possible sequence:

1. repeated intermediary / accounting / document habits;
2. unusual advance positioning before several "unexpected" changes;
3. links among secondary carriers / credit / information;
4. proof that selected events were nudged;
5. proof that the intended endpoint was wider conflict;
6. proof that Port reform was the strategic motive;
7. proof that the Villain later allowed Port suffering to continue.

This keeps the conspiracy discoverable without making it obvious from the beginning.
