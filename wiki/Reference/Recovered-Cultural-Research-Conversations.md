## Recovered chronology

### 2024-09 — Geography/environment as cultural pressure (summary only)
Early worldbuilding method emphasized land shape, weather, seasons, resource distribution, and environmental challenges. This is background methodology, not settled geography. See [Geography and Connections](../Geography-and-Connections.md), [World Rules](../World-Rules.md).

### 2024-10-20 — Early nomadic civilizations (summary only)
Burning Sands, Searing Plains, Crimson Horde, Grey Riders, Salt Flats communities; early pressure to settle, mobile trade, and the Convergence. See [Nomadic Peoples Research](Nomadic-Peoples-Recovered-Research.md) and current [Nomads](../Culture/Nomads.md). No claims about exact route, magic or borders are promoted.

### 2024-11-15 — Cultures organized by climate (names recovered)
Mongols, Kazakhs, Scythians, Huns, Tuvans, Nogais, Kalmyks and Kyrgyz. The historical and modern communities must not be conflated; see the nomadic research index. Original conversation prose unavailable.

### 2024-11-27 — Guilds, cults and institutional religion (summary only)
The user explored how actual associations and religious communities can organize **hierarchy, apprenticeships, monopolies, mutual aid, public authority, secrecy, specialist knowledge, charismatic leadership, isolation, ritual spectacle and dissent**. A list of historical/religious textual research leads was discussed: *Bardo Thodol*, *Sefer Yetzirah*, *Popol Vuh*, *Corpus Hermeticum*, *Pistis Sophia*, *Book of Enoch*, *Zhuangzi*, *Arbatel*, *Egyptian Book of the Dead*, *Atharva Veda*, *Dhammapada*, *Analects*, *Tao Te Ching*, *Manusmriti*, *Avesta*, *Tirukkural*, *Kojiki*, *Kalachakra Tantra*. These are **research leads with radically different genres, periods, languages and contexts**, not equivalent 'cults' or a shared teaching.

Retrospective memory also connects this conversation to early invented religion names: Woven Spiral, Song of Tides, Radiant Path, Veil of Echoes, Eternal Forge, Illuminated Circle, Silent Whisper, Harmonious [name truncated/unverified]. **Five of these (the Radiant Path, Eternal Forge, Illuminated Circle, Silent Whisper and Harmonious Path) are among the current nine faiths; Woven Spiral, Song of Tides and Veil of Echoes are not**; compare [Religion legacy volume](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md) with current [Religions](../Politics/Religions.md). Avoid interpreting sacred source texts as convenient occult-effect catalogs.

### 2024-12-15 to 2024-12-16 — A method for building a whole culture (summary only)
The user's working questions included:
- environment and historical experience;
- values, beliefs, ceremony and organized religion;
- communal food and meal rituals;
- how families, schools, guilds and oral narratives transmit knowledge;
- arts and material culture;
- ethics and disagreements;
- innovation, borrowing and continuity.

A historical 'Blaze' profile used a forge-centered landscape and developed **apprenticeship, forge-shrines, quenching rites, a Forgefire festival, bread baked with available heat, communal meals, hammer-sound music, and imported technical methods**. This is older brainstorming, **not** endorsement of the current geography, institutions or named festival.

### 2024-12-23 to 2024-12-27 — Regional deepening (summary only)
Older **Deepwood** discussion combined a forest-associated language label (Sylvanic), an elder assembly, living-tree/covered architecture, herbal work, oral storytelling, ceremonial tree communion, forest gatherings, and specialist trade. Older **Sunplain/Sunplains** discussion combined Mediterranean-style city-state models, a 'Sunian' language, courtyard/forum civic forms, caravan blessings, merchant oaths, mosaics, olive/wine/seafood cuisine and trade. **Port** proposals involved mixed neighborhoods, harbors, a coalition/municipal council, civic festivals, university-like scholarship and maritime governance.

**Disposition:** These are dated idea snapshots. Some older names, council composition, institutions, regional monopoly claims and exact festival arrangements were later superseded. Consult [Regional owners](../World-Overview.md), [Port](../Places/Port.md), [Source Register](Source-Register.md) and the preserved [Regional](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/02_Regions_Kingdoms.md) and [Architecture](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/03_Cities_Architecture.md) volumes.

### 2025-01 — Language, architecture and ordinary life (summary only)
Research questions included:
- language families, syntax, accents, slang, scripts, names;
- housing types and urban infrastructure;
- kinship, households, hospitality, mourning and rites;
- foods and preservation, dining etiquette, trade, work;
- city planning, education, medicine, transport, religious institutions;
- philosophy, customs and ethical differences **without claiming grammar determines intelligence or character**.

This survives more fully in [linguistics](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/07_Linguistics.md), [culture](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/04_Cultures_Peoples_Daily_Life.md), and current wiki owners.

### 2025-05 — Cultural second-order effects of political and economic disruption (summary only)
An older 'Operation Falling Scales' villain brainstorm involved a cascading sequence: foundry interruption → shortage and commodity speculation → maritime insecurity → religious interpretations → refugee flows and camps → credit, revolt and diplomacy. What matters to cultural research is **how labor, rumor, household displacement, trade, institutions and local rituals respond to crises**. Named villains, political outcomes and the exact sequence are legacy plot experiments; [Current Events](../Story/Current-Events.md) and [Main Conflict](../Story/Main-Conflict.md) own current story. Do not reintroduce old plot facts as evidence.

### 2025-10-14 to 2025-10-15 — Unusual human practices and expressive traditions (partial recovered summary)
A specific research thread sought exceptionally distinctive real-world cultural practices for worldbuilding, then focused especially on **voice as an instrument**, **storytelling**, **ritual**, and **technologies of memory**. Historical remembered examples:
- Inuit katajjaq / song contests and interlocking vocal play;
- BaAka/Mbuti interlocking/hocket vocal styles;
- Georgian and Sardinian polyphony;
- Tuvan throat singing;
- Polynesian navigation and Australian Aboriginal songlines;
- Tana Toraja mortuary customs;
- Yoruba Ifá knowledge systems, Egungun, talking drums;
- Trobriand Kula exchange;
- Northwest Coast potlatch;
- Ainu iyomante;
- Inka khipu / quipu, wampum, whistled languages;
- Garamantes foggara irrigation; Balinese subak;
- Indonesian wayang shadow theatre;
- Malta Hypogeum acoustics.

**Strong caveat:** This is not a recovered verbatim transcript or proof of every detail once discussed. Several past answer formulations may have been oversimplified or misleading (for example 'all Inuit do song duels'; 'the Tana Toraja live with the dead'; 'Dogon cosmology proves astronomical secrets'; or 'all Aboriginal cultures use identical songlines'). The [practice research](Cultural-Practices-and-Institutions.md) validates only named selected mechanisms with linked sources and lists the rest as **open research leads**.

### 2026-03-21 to 2026-03-22 — Culture and environment (summary only)
Conceptual research terms included **cultural ecology, ecological anthropology, adaptation, habitus, and cultural tightness**, with examples of Inuit ice/weather expertise, Japanese cormorant fishing and Dutch waterworks. Use cautiously: pressure and environment can influence institutions and skills; they do not fix a people's psychology or character. See [World Rules](../World-Rules.md).

### 2026-09-22 to 2026-09-25 — Archive migration and cultural diffusion method
The repo preserved all 220 original notes and created the current wiki. The later framework emphasizes **historical contact rather than region-color mosaics**. Mechanisms include trade routes, intermarriage, labor migration, displaced families, apprentices, faith networks, craft technology and settlement layers. Discussion also tested **local community → specific analogue → mechanism → exclusions → history → neighboring contact** as a better model than simply calling a whole region 'Celtic' or 'Asian'.

There was a retrospective estimate that roughly **36–40 individual community profiles** might be useful, including three different mobile circuits. This was **a proposed research target**, not a canon population count. Current [Cultural Inspiration](../Culture/Culture-and-Influence.md), [Social Fabric](../Social-Fabric.md), [Culture](../Culture/Regional-Social-Dynamics.md) and [Movement and Information](../Movement-and-Information.md) own the principles.

### 2026-09-23 — Architectural and culinary contact-zone examples (recorded notes)
Older notes explained that a household of migrants may preserve **room arrangement and privacy customs** while adopting local stone, fire codes and labor patterns. A North Coast salt-fish method plus Low Rivers vegetable/pickling methods could become a new local dish over generations. **These are examples of a proposed diffusion method**, not fixed regional menus. See [Architecture](../Culture/Architecture.md) and [Food Diaspora](../Culture/Food-Diaspora-and-Adaptation.md).
