# Community-to-Analogue Matrix — V1

## Status

**Active research-routing matrix — not canon.**

Purpose:

> move comparative cultural research from the **region level** to the **actual community level**.

This page implements:

- [Cultural Analogue Coverage Assessment — V1](Cultural-Analogue-Coverage-Assessment-V1.md);
- [Mobile Peoples Comparative Research — V1](Mobile-Peoples-Comparative-Research-V1.md);
- [Mobile Circuits — Working Profiles V1](Mobile-Circuits-Working-Profiles-V1.md).

## Rule

A comparator is not:

> "this community is fantasy X."

A comparator is:

> "this real-world case helps answer one specific social / ecological / institutional question."

Every final community should combine:

- multiple research sources;
- local 10Kings history;
- neighboring influence;
- internal disagreement.

# 13. Research completion rule

A row reaches **R2** only when it has:

1. at least 2 useful comparative cases;
2. each source has a specific mechanism;
3. explicit non-borrows;
4. enough evidence to avoid stereotype.

A row reaches **P1** only when it has:

1. local history;
2. livelihood;
3. household pattern;
4. law / institution;
5. internal disagreement;
6. neighboring influence;
7. one cultural transmission output.

A row reaches **P2** only after stress testing against:

- ecology;
- politics;
- economy;
- naming;
- story use;
- cultural bleed.
