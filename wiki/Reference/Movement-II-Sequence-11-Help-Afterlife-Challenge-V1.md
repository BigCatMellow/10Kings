## Summary

**The sequence passes structurally. Three presentation choices need bounded correction.**

What works:

- one Low Rivers household survives an immediate debt deadline because the opening shipment actually settled;
- abundance still harms nearby producers;
- the Records Worker accumulates documentary familiarity rather than solving the Council;
- the Port pin order arrives in Stone Hills as an ordinary civilian request trapped behind priority work.

The needed corrections are about **symmetry, suspicion, and explanation**.

---

# 1. Low Rivers loser should not feel paired by the author

V1 places the losing household directly across the lane from the helped household.

That is plausible, but a little neat.

Better:

- keep them in the same market / district;
- make the connection social but not physically mirrored;
- perhaps the helped household hears about the sale through:
  - market witness;
  - cooperative contact;
  - water / village meeting;
  - shared carrier.

The key contrast is:

> same abundant harvest, different access to time.

It does not need to happen literally next door.

---

# 2. The land sale should remain an ordinary distressed choice

Keep:

- seller needs liquidity;
- buyer has cash;
- price is bad;
- no legal seizure is required.

Do not imply:

- formal foreclosure procedure;
- predatory law;
- one villainous land buyer.

The structural point is stronger if the transaction is legal and comprehensible.

---

# 3. Records Worker should recognize, not investigate

V1:

> "That was worth remembering."

This is slightly too active.

Better:

- the mark feels familiar;
- the worker copies it correctly;
- perhaps notices the same wording / house appeared before;
- then moves on.

Later evidence should emerge because:

> records accumulate,

not because the worker begins privately building a case in Sequence 11.

---

# 4. Stone Hills grievance must pre-exist the Port order

The Port pin request should **land inside an argument already underway**.

That prevents:

> one small Port order causes Stone Hills labor unrest.

Instead:

~~~text
existing strategic-priority backlog / overtime / secrecy tension
+
another ordinary order displaced
→ Port request becomes one more visible example
~~~

This fits the current-events owner.

---

# 5. End Stone Hills on the displaced order

V1 explains:

> somebody else will wait longer.

We should see that instead.

Strong ending class:

- clerk inserts Port pins;
- removes / pushes another ordinary repair slip;
- reads who it belongs to;
- hesitates;
- updates date.

Stop there.

The reader understands allocation.
