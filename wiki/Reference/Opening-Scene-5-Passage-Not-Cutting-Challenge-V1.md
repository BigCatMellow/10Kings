## Summary

**The legal / cultural mechanism passes. Wurdren needs one visible correction.**

V1 succeeds at:

- three real interests;
- layered Longwood rights;
- folklore performing jurisdictional work;
- Port certifying records without claiming Longwood jurisdiction;
- narrowing the conflict rather than remotely solving it;
- creating a plausible reason for onward travel.

Main weakness:

> Wurdren asks almost every useful question correctly on the first attempt.

That risks turning:

> good listener

into:

> hidden legal savant.

---

# 1. Give Wurdren one wrong frame

Early in the dispute, have him ask something like:

> **"Who owns the ground beside the road?"**

The Longwood delegate should correct him:

> **"Wrong question. Right to do what?"**

This correction can then produce the actual categories:

- pass;
- cut;
- gather;
- drain;
- widen;
- use water.

Wurdren should accept the correction immediately.

That is the character strength.

Not:

> he already understands layered forest law.

But:

> **he can abandon a bad question once someone shows him why it is bad.**

---

# 2. Strengthen the invitation logic

The final invitation should not imply:

> Wurdren is now the obvious expert mediator.

Better reasons:

- he witnessed what Port actually established;
- he can attest that the commercial copy was incomplete for the cutting question;
- he kept the room from becoming physical;
- neither local faction owns him;
- he already heard both sides state their claims.

The delegate can want:

> **a witness to the narrowing, not a savior.**

That is stronger.

---

# 3. Make timing less mechanically convenient

V1's:

> four days until crews reach the disputed sections

is useful but a little crisp.

Use uncertainty:

- four days if work stays ahead;
- six if a lower section delays;
- local party can return in three / four.

The key is:

> there is still time for human intervention,

not:

> the plot produced a perfect countdown.

---

# 4. Delegate trust

Keep initial distrust.

At the end, the delegate should still not fully trust Wurdren.

A stronger invitation line:

> **"You heard what was actually agreed here. Come hear what people say it meant once we get home."**

This makes the relationship continue through evidence / trust rather than instant liking.

---

**PASS.**

The Turning should remain a small cultural aperture.

This preserves Port's limited jurisdiction perfectly.

Do not strengthen it into a judgment.
