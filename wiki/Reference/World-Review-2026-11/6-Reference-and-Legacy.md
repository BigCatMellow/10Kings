1. All 13 Reference pages have a status block, but under five different headings and labels. Original-Cultural-Systems-Batch-2 calls itself "PROVISIONAL", which is a canon label. Eight cultural-research pages restate one method six times and repeat about a dozen case mechanisms.

3. The Source Register is stale on D24/D25 (named gods, named guilds, the Overpath, Seven Hill and the Ridge-Roamers are now provisional). It also has two sections numbered "8", and its status line still says it covers only the 2026-09-22 pass. Story-Legacy-Research-Library cites the wrong file for the Port factions and says two things were declined that weren't. Recovered-Conversations says five of the current faiths are "not current religions".
4. The 15 at-risk legacy items: the Underpass's everyday life, the Council's founding arc and tradecraft, the Circle of Mirrors, five regional "founding wounds", the whole flora/fauna/ecology pass (no wiki owner exists), Port's internal factions and slang, the children's rhymes and wedding customs, syncretic faiths, sects, per-faith current events, the creation myth, the Villain-plan opinion tables, and the logbook POV.
5. Main insight: older promotions turned specific lines into generic bullet lists, so the legacy notes still hold the concrete, spoken texture that D14 and D22 ask for. Promote quotes and mechanisms, not categories.

## Insights

1. **Promotion has been lossy in one direction.** The older passes turned concrete lines into category lists. "Witnessed by whoever's sober" became "which ritual occurs first"; the Underpass's torch shifts and alarm drums became "Settlement may include: miners; guides…". D14 and D22 ask for the opposite. The legacy notes are now the repository's main store of the specific, spoken texture the wiki lacks. Proposed rule for future reconciliation: *promote the line and the mechanism, never only the category.* This would change how all fifteen items in Q2 get imported.

2. **The Council's founding answers the ending.** Vol 08 says the Council began, before the Convergence, as an idealist and more open body that the guilds and kings drove into the shadows. Main-Conflict's resolution has the Council "losing secrecy and unilateral authority". If the founding is adopted, that ending becomes a *return* rather than a defeat, and an openness faction inside the six houses explains why exposure doesn't simply destroy the Council. It also settles the open question of the Council's age (lean: pre-Convergence, Provisional).

3. **The Circle of Mirrors is the Council's version of a nail.** D21 forbids the Council from acting as a named body in the stories. Unwitting critics give it a presence without action: a Ridge-Roamer ballad keeper, or a Bardic Circle performer, whose remarks a stranger writes down. The reader can sense the pattern; nobody in the story can. This gives the Council side of the Domino Map story material it currently doesn't have.

4. **After D25, the faiths need friction, and the legacy notes already supply it.** Shared gods (Montara between Weaver and Forge), syncretic fusions in Port, sects (Forgewardens) and the Forge Trials all make "faiths span borders" visible without magic. The Forge's relighting rite forcing contracts to be renegotiated is a world-story or domino premise that needs no new canon. The main risk is the Fell Pantheon: it should come in, if at all, only as *what one faith accuses another of*.

5. **The ecology gap will bite the stories first.** The wiki has no owner for flora or fauna. The 2026-09-24 pass already ran the stress tests (forage systems, corridors, no crossbreeding, kelp traded inland rather than grown there). Without an owner, each story invents its own animals and plants, against World Rule 1. One narrow Provisional page would fix it cheaply.

6. **Founding wounds give the dominoes something to press.** Northwind's "breaker of the freeze" taboo sits right beside the current-events thread "Fish stocks and access" and the Northwind character-domino (Maris Bleakshore). The Villain's leverage works best where it makes a region's own "never again" turn against it. Five regions lack this memory today.

7. **Documents are the setting's natural carriers of patterns.** The harbor logbook POV, the D3/D25 ship's-lantern paper and Prototype A's tally sticks all let the reader see what characters can't (D21). A Sunday Morning story told partly as ledger entries would test this.

8. **About 13,000 words of comparative research have produced nothing adopted.** The prototypes are better used as D23 world-story seeds (a story set at the Orchard Forecast Table) than as candidate canon. That gives them a use without raising them to canon.

2. **The Fell Pantheon.** Leave it out, or keep it only as heresy accusations between faiths? *Lean:* accusations only, with no cults, powers or undead.
