# Sunplains Recipes

## Status

**Provisional examples consistent with [Food](../Food.md).**

Sunplains cuisine should vary by city, coast, irrigation district, faith and class. “Refined” food is not the diet of every household.

## Method profile

Common techniques:

- clay-pot braising;
- griddle and stone-oven flatbreads;
- open-air charcoal or orchard-wood grilling;
- oil-based cooking;
- sun drying;
- fruit, herb and vinegar sauces;
- pulses and grain dishes;
- layered and syruped festival pastry.

### 1. Olive Flatbread and Soft Cheese

**Typical ingredients**
- wheat/barley flour;
- water;
- yeast/starter or unleavened versions;
- olive-like oil;
- chopped olives where available;
- soft goat/sheep cheese;
- herbs.

**In-world method**
1. Dough is mixed and rested.
2. Thin rounds cook on a griddle or hot oven floor.
3. Oil is brushed on after baking.
4. Cheese, olives and herbs are served alongside rather than always baked in.

---

### 2. Orchard-Pear Clay-Pot Braise

**Typical ingredients**
- lamb, goat or poultry;
- onion;
- pear or quince-like fruit;
- oil;
- cinnamon-like bark;
- cumin-like seed;
- saffron-like luxury spice only in wealthy versions;
- honey;
- broth.

**Method**
1. Meat is lightly browned in the clay pot.
2. Onion and spice cook in the oil.
3. Meat, fruit and a modest amount of broth are layered.
4. The covered pot sits over low heat so trapped steam does most of the work.
5. Honey is added late.
6. It is served with bread or grain.

---

### 3. Honey-Drizzled Nut Pastries

**Typical ingredients**
- thin rolled dough;
- nuts;
- honey;
- spicebark;
- butter or oil.

**Method**
1. Dough is rolled or stretched thin.
2. Layers are brushed with fat and filled with nuts.
3. The pastry bakes crisp in a communal or household oven.
4. Warm honey syrup is poured over after baking.

Festival versions can be elaborate; everyday versions are simpler folded pastries.

---

### 4. Grain and Herb Salad

Cooked cracked wheat, barley or couscous-like granules are cooled and tossed with cucumber-like vegetables, herbs, onion, oil, sour fruit/vinegar and dried or fresh orchard fruit.

---

### 5. Herb-Crusted Coastal Fish

Fish is rubbed with oil, herbs and coarse grain crumbs, then baked in a clay dish or grilled over clean coals. Sour fruit, vinegar or pomegranate-like reduction finishes the dish.

---

### 6. Fava-Like Bean Purée

Cooked pulses are mashed with oil, garlic-like allium, salt and sour fruit/vinegar. It is eaten with flatbread and raw or grilled vegetables.

---

### 7. Orchard Pilaf

Rice where trade and irrigation support it, or local grain elsewhere, is toasted in oil and cooked with broth, nuts and dried fruit. Wealthier versions use expensive aromatic spice.
