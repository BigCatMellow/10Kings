# Recipe Working Set

## Status

**Provisional culinary working set.**

[Food](../Food.md) remains the canonical owner for regional culinary ecology. [Food Diaspora and Adaptation](../Food-Diaspora-and-Adaptation.md) owns the rules for migration, substitution and hybridization.

The recipes collected here turn those principles into concrete dishes for writing, worldbuilding and scene work. A named recipe does **not** become established canon merely because it appears here.

## Design rules

Every recipe in this folder should obey the following:

- food follows ecology, trade, class, season, preservation, religion and history;
- a regional cuisine is a family of habits, not one menu;
- fuel and cookware matter;
- preservation methods should solve an actual environmental or logistical problem;
- border dishes should combine neighboring traditions through repeated contact rather than arbitrary “fusion”;
- real-world dishes are inspiration pools, not one-to-one cultural copies;
- ingredients that do not yet exist in the setting remain functional analogues rather than hard canon;
- unsafe brainstorm details are not retained as culinary practice.

## How to read these recipes

Each entry distinguishes three layers:

**Cultural logic** — why this kind of dish exists.

**In-world method** — how a local household, work crew, inn, caravan or feast kitchen would actually prepare it.

**Recipe frame** — a usable ingredient-and-process model. Quantities are intentionally approximate where household variation should remain normal.

## Working ingredient language

Where earlier notes used modern or over-specific terms, this set prefers setting-compatible categories:

- hardy grain rather than requiring one modern cultivar;
- sour fruit or vinegar rather than assuming citrus everywhere;
- aromatic seed/bark/root rather than importing a whole modern spice pantry;
- mountain livestock rather than assuming a single species in every district;
- orchard fruit rather than making every valley grow the same fruit.

That keeps the dishes recognizable while leaving room for local ecology and class variation.
