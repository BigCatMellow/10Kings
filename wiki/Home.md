# Two Sons Wiki

This is the working knowledge base for the setting currently called **Two Sons**.

## Core idea

Two Sons is a low-fantasy world built around **interdependence**. Regions differ because of climate, history, geography, language, institutions, and cultural memory, but they are not monocultures or resource caricatures. Stone Hills has more mining; that does not mean everyone is a miner. Longwood contains major forests; that does not mean every person is a mystic ranger. Each region contains farms, towns, crafts, elites, poor laborers, religious communities, criminals, migrants, minorities, and internal disagreements.

The story grows from the collision of three scales:

- **The Council** works at the systemic level, quietly steering the economy to preserve a balance it believes is necessary.
- **The Villain** works strategically against that order, arranging chains of events that can destabilize kingdoms and create the opening he needs.
- **Wurdren** works at the human level, responding to the people harmed by both systems and schemes.

The setting should make it possible to follow one damaged bridge, missing caravan, bad harvest, guild strike, rumor, religious dispute, or shipping delay outward until it touches multiple regions and factions.
