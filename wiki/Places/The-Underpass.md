# The Underpass

## Status

**Working canon.**

The Underpass are a network of natural caves, fault passages, excavated tunnels, chambers, settlements, and trade routes beneath portions of Central Range.

It is not a single road and not wholly mapped.

## Environment

Conditions vary:
- dry stone corridors;
- flooded sections;
- underground streams;
- large caverns;
- unstable faults;
- bad-air pockets;
- mine workings;
- inhabited junctions;
- sealed or sacred spaces.

Some routes are wide enough for pack animals or small carts. Others require single-file travel or climbing.

## Settlement

Communities in the Underpass may include:
- miners;
- guides;
- traders;
- people displaced by surface wars;
- religious communities;
- fugitives;
- craftspeople;
- families born underground.

Settlements form around:
- water;
- ventilation;
- stable caverns;
- route intersections;
- mines;
- defensible choke points.

## Economy

Legal trade and illicit trade overlap.

Economic activities include:
- guide services;
- tolls;
- mining;
- storage;
- caravan lodging;
- fungi and subterranean food production;
- repair;
- smuggling.

## Politics

Authority is fragmented.

Possible governing units:
- enclave councils;
- guide families;
- mining fraternities;
- local militias;
- trade coalitions;
- criminal organizations.

Surface kingdoms claim some entrances and sections but rarely control the entire network.

## Current events

- rival enclaves are fighting over profitable corridors;
- new mineral finds have intensified claims;
- collapses have redirected trade;
- smugglers are exploiting official route closures;
- strange animal activity may indicate ecological disruption rather than supernatural monsters;
- Council agents seek reliable route influence;
- the Villain can use the network to move people and goods where surface observers do not expect them.

## Story function

The Underpass physically embodies the setting's theme: the visible political map is not the same as the real network through which people, goods, and power move.
