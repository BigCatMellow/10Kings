# Seven Hill

## Status

**Provisional.** bt 7274 named it on 2026-11-04: "Seven Hill (the former military base turned town)". That's all that's settled. It isn't placed on the map, and nothing else about it is decided.

## What the wiki already has that bears on it

None of this is settled as Seven Hill's history. It is what a story or a ruling would build from:

- [Social Fabric](../Social-Fabric.md#mixed-communities-create-local-culture) lists "old military settlements" among the places where a local culture forms that residents defend against both of the cultures it came from.
- The [Spine](The-Spine.md#physical-role) holds "old fortifications" and "strategic fortresses", and pre-Convergence [Highridge](../Regions/Highridge-Plateau.md#historical-identity) had "fortress communities" before toll warfare gave way to negotiated passage.
- The [Convergence](../History/The-Convergence.md#border-recognition) could define boundaries by "old forts", and [Port](Port.md#neutrality)'s charter limits kingdom troops inside the city.

## Still open

- **Where it is,** and whose base it was: a kingdom's, a pass lord's, a city's, a coalition's.
- **When and why it stopped being a base:** a treaty, a peace, a drawdown, a debt, a move of the border.
- **What's left of the base in the town:** walls, a parade ground, barracks turned houses, a mess hall, a bell, a drill habit, a way of talking.
- **Who lives there now:** old soldiers' families, the people who supplied the base, people who came after.

Stories that want it are on [Story Seeds](../Sunday-Morning/Stories/Story-Seeds.md#seven-hill).
