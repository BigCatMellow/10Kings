# Seven Hill

## Status

**Provisional.** bt 7274 named it on 2026-11-04: "Seven Hill (the former military base turned town)". The same day he ruled the rest of this section ([D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)).

## What it is

Seven Hill was a fortress on one of the [Spine](The-Spine.md)'s passes, on the [Highridge](../Regions/Highridge-Plateau.md) side. It was emptied when Highridge's toll warfare gave way to negotiated passage, and it has been a town for generations since. The base is the town's old bones: what was built for soldiers is lived in by their families' families and by the people who came after.

## What the wiki already has that bears on it

None of this is settled as Seven Hill's history. It is what a story or a ruling would build from:

- [Social Fabric](../Social-Fabric.md#mixed-communities-create-local-culture) lists "old military settlements" among the places where a local culture forms that residents defend against both of the cultures it came from.
- The [Spine](The-Spine.md#physical-role) holds "old fortifications" and "strategic fortresses", and pre-Convergence [Highridge](../Regions/Highridge-Plateau.md#historical-identity) had "fortress communities" before toll warfare gave way to negotiated passage.
- The [Convergence](../History/The-Convergence.md#border-recognition) could define boundaries by "old forts", and [Port](Port.md#neutrality)'s charter limits kingdom troops inside the city.

## What the legacy notes say

Nothing, by name. A search of the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/README.md) and every branch found no Seven Hill and no former base turned town. The nearest thing is a one-line brainstorm, Stonefort, "a heavily fortified town built into the cliffs … ruled by a military governor" ([Volume 03](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/03_Cities_Architecture.md)), which is a working fort, not a former one. So Seven Hill is new with bt 7274.

## Still open

- **Which pass,** and which pass lord or toll town held the fortress.
- **How long ago** the garrison left, against the Convergence (before it, or as part of it).
- **What's left of the base in the town:** walls, a parade ground, barracks turned houses, a mess hall, a bell, a drill habit, a way of talking. [Story Seeds](../Sunday-Morning/Stories/Story-Seeds.md#seven-hill) proposes a bell and walls carted off for houses; neither is settled.
- **Who lives there now,** and whether the town still sees itself as a garrison of something.

Stories that want it are on [Story Seeds](../Sunday-Morning/Stories/Story-Seeds.md#seven-hill).
