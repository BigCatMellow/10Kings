# Religions

## Status

**Working canon for the nine faiths and their cores; their working life and gods are provisional.** Nine faiths that cross borders, never one per kingdom, are working canon. Each faith's **Working life** came in as provisional by [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions) and its **Gods** by [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions). *(Labelled in the [2026-11 review](../Reference/World-Review-2026-11.md#status-labels).)*

The world currently contains nine major trans-regional traditions. They do not map neatly onto kingdoms.

Each faith should have local schools, reform movements, institutions, charities, political interests, and internal disputes.

Some communities keep old relics said to answer honesty or care; whether any of them does anything is never confirmed. See [Magic and Relics](../Magic.md#relics).

## 1. The Cyclic Order

Core: existence moves through cycles of birth, death, renewal, time, balance, and moral consequence.

Institutional interests:
- calendars;
- rites of passage;
- mediation;
- burial;
- seasonal observance.

Current scheme:
A reform movement argues that post-Convergence stability has become stagnation. Some members want deliberate social disruption to "complete" overdue cycles; traditional elders oppose them.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-cyclic-order)): Circular temples with a great wheel for the calendar at the center; priests called Guides of the Cycle, and elders whom councils consult as mediators. Its rites mark the turns of a life: at a naming the parents light a spiral candle, at a wedding two threads are braided into one, at a death the body is laid in a round tomb and mourners trace spirals in sand or water. It keeps a sacred almanac of good times for large decisions, and its scripture, the Spiral Codex, is written in rings.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): Chronospect (time, a many-eyed turning wheel), Omnia (totality), Equilibria (balance), Aevum (eternity, a river round the world) and Mortalis (mortality, half bone and half light).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D35](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [the Count Stones](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-count-stones).

## 2. Path of the Great Weaver

Core: beings, places, ancestors, animals, and actions are threads in a living weave.

Institutional interests:
- stewardship;
- oral history;
- ancestry;
- ecological obligations;
- community memory.

Current scheme:
Networks across several regions are documenting land and water damage, creating a trans-regional moral case against large extraction/infrastructure projects. Some Council families see this as a threat to economic planning.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-path-of-the-great-weaver)): Seasonal rites: planting blessings, midsummer fires, autumn thanks and a winter vigil for the ancestors. Families keep small outdoor shrines to their dead with flowers, food and woven tokens, and some keepers seek the ancestors' guidance through dreams, chants and herbs (a belief, as [Magic](../Magic.md#everything-else-that-has-been-claimed) holds). A tree is planted for every tree felled; the river is cleaned each year in a day of song; pilgrims leave small carvings at stone circles. Craft is devotion, and its teachings live in oral chronicles and in sayings carved in wood or stone.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): spirits of the weave rather than gods apart from it: Arborealus (the forest), Montara (the mountains), Flumenara (rivers), Zephyria (wind) and Viridia (growth).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D35](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [The One Tree](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-one-tree).

## 3. The Radiant Path

Core: moral life is struggle toward courage, justice, truth, and wisdom against cruelty, lies, and discord.

Institutional interests:
- charity;
- public ethics;
- justice;
- moral testimony.

Current scheme:
Different branches disagree over whether hidden Council rule is a necessary compromise or fundamentally a form of darkness through deception.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-radiant-path)): Weekly worship in temples set to the suns' path, opened at dawn by lighting a beacon flame; clergy called Luminaries. Its charity is organized: collections for the poor, sanctuaries for the sick, and volunteer bands who stand up for the vulnerable. Pilgrims seek places of natural light: sunlit cliffs, caverns where light breaks through crystal, fields of fireflies. An all-night vigil honors those who upheld the light, and at the renewal flame people write their fears or failures and burn them.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): aspects of one Eternal Radiance: Justitia (justice), Valorus (courage), Luxphera (light and truth) and Concordia (harmony).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D36](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [the Dawn Hill](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-dawn-hill).

## 4. The Eternal Forge

Core: creation and destruction are linked; character and society are shaped through disciplined transformation.

Institutional interests:
- craft;
- apprenticeship;
- ethical labor;
- rebuilding.

Current scheme:
Its guild-adjacent institutions are becoming active in labor and quality disputes, potentially turning a spiritual tradition into a cross-regional political force.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-eternal-forge)): Every workshop keeps a small shrine with a fire that is never let go out. Smiths pray before lighting a forge, bless tools with oil and flame, and name and dedicate a great work. Once a year every fire in a town is put out and relit together, with a public showing of work and contests of craft. When a tool is worn out it is broken and melted down so it can become something else, and rebuilding after a disaster counts as devotion. Its ethic, the Artisan's Code, asks for honest workmanship and humility in mastery.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): Vulcanestra (fire and transformation), Montara (earth and stone, shared with the Weaver), Fortificus (strength and endurance) and Artesius (craft and invention).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D35](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [the Burning Hill](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-burning-hill); also [the Star Iron](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-star-iron).

## 5. The Illuminated Circle

Core: knowledge, reason, inquiry, and intellectual freedom are sacred values.

Institutional interests:
- libraries;
- schools;
- debate;
- archives;
- public reasoning.

Current scheme:
Scholars are collecting contradictory trade and government records. Some may be close to proving systematic manipulation without yet understanding the Council.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-illuminated-circle)): Its worship is argument: gatherings for debate, contests of reasoning, and councils where a community's dilemma is set out and reasoned through together. Libraries are its holy places, kept by librarians, and people mark a discovery by writing it into a shared book. A mentorship begins when the teacher hands the student a quill and ends when the student argues against the teacher before the circle. Scholars go on teaching journeys to found schools, and solstices and eclipses are watched as ceremonies.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): Omnia (all knowledge, with book and quill), Liberion (freedom of thought), Epistemia (inquiry) and Logos (reason).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D36](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [the Argument House](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-argument-house).

## 6. The Silent Whisper

Core: silence, uncertainty, hidden truths, patience, and introspection.

Institutional interests:
- preservation;
- contemplation;
- secret archives;
- lost knowledge.

Current scheme:
Some custodians believe dangerous truths should be withheld until society can survive them. Others argue that this has become complicity with elite secrecy.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-the-silent-whisper)): Listening meditation, fasts of silence, and the practice of writing a thought down and destroying it. Pilgrims walk at night to dark or quiet places, some without a lantern, and to stones where the acoustics carry the faintest sound. Its charity is anonymous: food or help left without a name. Keepers hold teachings that are passed on only when someone is ready, under an oath of silence, and its scripture is written in riddles.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): Noxiquill (secrets and the night), Silentus (stillness), Mysterion (paradox) and Arcanus (hidden knowledge).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D35](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [the Tide Road](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-tide-road); its pilgrims also walk to [the Black Pool](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-black-pool) and, by lean, [the Mother's Cradle](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-mothers-cradle).

## 7. The Harmonious Path

Core: social responsibility, service, restraint, conflict resolution, and balance.

Institutional interests:
- mediation;
- community welfare;
- nonviolent conflict settlement.

Current scheme:
Local Harmonious communities are building parallel mutual-aid and arbitration systems where official institutions have failed, unintentionally creating political power.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-harmonious-path)): A yearly day of public kindness, with feasts honoring the community's peacemakers; lanterns set on water at night for peace; and festivals of harmony that mark a harvest, a treaty or a reconciliation by mending tools, planting trees and eating together. Its knot and its balancing scales turn up in courts and meeting halls. Three tendencies run through it: withdrawn communities devoted to stillness, reformers who work in public, and wandering mediators.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): Concordia (unity and community), Harmonia (balance), Serenica (inner peace) and Temperantia (moderation).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D36](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [the Reconciliation Bridge](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-reconciliation-bridge).

## 8. The Dual Flame

Core: moral integrity comes from understanding and balancing constructive and destructive impulses.

Institutional interests:
- counseling;
- confession;
- mentorship;
- personal discipline.

Current scheme:
Its counselors are seeing similar fear, anger, and radicalization patterns across regions and may recognize that separate crises share a source before political leaders do.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-dual-flame)): Followers look back on the day at dawn and dusk, and may write down their wrongs and burn the page. A yearly festival lights twin bonfires; an overnight vigil around one fire is for telling one's struggles and renewing vows; at the equinox there is silence and then a shared feast. Its temples are built in two halves, one light and one dark. It has a keeper tradition that tends sacred fires, a stricter reforming tradition, and a controversial one that holds the dark must be explored to be understood.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): Thalos (discipline), Sereia (passion), Valoron (courage and honor), Nefari (the tempter) and Lysivere (the balancekeeper, holding both flames).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D36](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [the Rocking Stone](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-rocking-stone).

## 9. The Infinite Compass

Core: life is journey, direction, exploration, and self-discovery.

Institutional interests:
- pilgrimage;
- maps;
- hospitality;
- travelers;
- road shrines.

Current scheme:
Its pilgrim networks move information across borders faster than many governments expect. The Council, the Villain, spies, and criminals all have reasons to influence these networks.

**Working life** (provisional, [D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); from the [legacy notes](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md#src-religion-infinite-compass)): Before a large decision a follower turns a full circle, facing every road. Communities map their journeys together, outward and inward, and on a yearly day of departure they send off those who are leaving with blessings and gifts. Waystones and road shrines carry its spiral; sailors bless a family's old compass before a voyage. Its keepers preserve maps and travel lore, its seekers go where no one has been, and some followers make the journey inward instead.

**Gods** (provisional, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions); names from the legacy notes): Navigos (paths and guidance), Labyrintus (lost paths and mysteries), Infinis (the horizon) and Inventura (curiosity).

**Holy site** (provisional, [D35](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)): [the Salt Mirror](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-salt-mirror).

## Working life and gods

Each faith's **Working life** above came in from the legacy notes ([Volume 06](../../legacy-notes/2026-09-22/consolidated-package/06_Religion_Gods_Artifacts.md)) as provisional on 2026-11-04, at bt 7274's ruling ([D24](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)). The legacy assigns each faith to one kingdom; that was left out, because the rule below forbids it. The legacy's section on religious artifacts is answered by [Magic and Relics](../Magic.md#relics).

**The faiths have gods** (bt 7274, 2026-11-04, [D25](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)). Each faith's **Gods** line above gives the legacy pantheon as provisional working names. The gods are what the faithful believe in. Like relics and omens, they are never confirmed: no god appears, speaks or acts on the page, and the [Magic](../Magic.md#writing-rule) writing rule applies to prayers and answered prayers as much as to relics. Things to know when using them:

- **Some gods are shared.** Montara belongs to both the Weaver and the Forge (the legacy says so), Omnia to both the Cyclic Order and the Illuminated Circle, and Concordia to both the Radiant and the Harmonious Path. Faiths that cross borders can share a god and disagree about it.
- **Two names nearly match:** the Radiant Path's Valorus and the Dual Flame's Valoron, both courage. Kept as two, since two faiths may name one virtue differently; a story shouldn't put them side by side until that is settled.
- **The names are Latinate working names.** Personal and place names follow [Naming](../Culture/Naming.md); whether the gods' names do is still open.

**Readings from the [2026-11 review](../Reference/World-Review-2026-11.md#insights)** (leans, provisional):

- **The gods' names are a shared liturgical register.** Nine faiths cross three mutually unintelligible language families and share gods, which fits Latinate names kept in a prestigious, half-understood religious register, used beside local names in each family ([Language Families](../Language-Families-and-Contact.md)). This answers whether the gods' names follow Naming: they don't, because they aren't from any one people.
- **Writing and burning is older than the faiths.** The Radiant Path burns written fears at the renewal flame, the Dual Flame burns a page of wrongs, and the Silent Whisper writes a thought and destroys it. Read as one folk practice that three faiths absorbed and each claims as its own ([Folk Belief](../Culture/Folk-Belief-Taboo-and-Superstition.md)), it is evidence of a shared folk religion beneath the faiths and a rite mixed-faith households already share. It needs literacy and paper, so it is probably marked by class.
- **Faiths that cross borders mix.** The legacy notes propose syncretic fusions emerging in Port (a Radiance-Forged faith, a Compass-Circle alliance, a Cycle-Weaver practice) and sects inside faiths (Flamekeepers, Forgewardens, Icebound Seekers, Glacial Watchers), with craft as prayer ("every net cast is a petition"). They are unpromoted, in the [backlog](../Reference/README.md#legacy-reconciliation-backlog).
- **The legacy's Fell Pantheon** (soul-binding, undead) does not come in. If it appears at all, it is only as what one faith accuses another of: heresy talk, with no cults, powers or undead ([Magic](../Magic.md)).
- **The faiths collect but never conclude, in the stories.** Four faiths' current schemes come close to the pattern: the Illuminated Circle comparing records, the Dual Flame's counselors, Compass pilgrims carrying news, the Weaver documenting damage. In the Sunday Morning stories they gather and never conclude on the page ([D21](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)); the main story can let them conclude later.

## The faiths and the sky

**Provisional** ([D51](../Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#the-authors-decisions)). These are leans for bt 7274 to overrule. They take the sky from [Sky, Seasons and Sea](../Sky-Seasons-and-Sea.md): a close pair of suns, gold and red, with two shadows, a second dusk and a regular crossing when one passes the other; at least one moon; auroras in the north; falling stars. Every faith sees the same sky and reads it differently. As with the gods, these are beliefs: the sky never confirms one faith against another ([Magic](../Magic.md#writing-rule)).

| Faith | What it sees | What it does with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cyclic Order](#1-the-cyclic-order) | the turning itself: day, the crossing, the moon, the solstices; Chronospect's wheel made visible | keeps the count, and names the dates everyone else borrows. At the [Count Stones](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-count-stones), the "shadows of two stones meet a third" are read as both suns' shadows meeting on the year's turning days |
| [Great Weaver](#2-path-of-the-great-weaver) | the sky as part of the weave: the moon pulling the sea, the auroras as threads of the dead | no sky-worship; the dead are honored on Aurora Nights where the faiths overlap in Northwind |
| [Radiant Path](#3-the-radiant-path) | the gold sun as the Eternal Radiance, Luxphera's light | dawn rites and vigils ([the Dawn Hill](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-dawn-hill)). In a crossing, when the light dims, the faithful keep a flame lit until it returns |
| [Eternal Forge](#4-the-eternal-forge) | the red sun as the world's banked coal; falling stars as sparks off the anvil | the [Star Iron](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-star-iron), a fallen star, is its proof of the forge above. Smiths light new fires at the red sun's rising |
| [Illuminated Circle](#5-the-illuminated-circle) | a sky that can be measured | records the crossings and argues over predicting them, the nearest thing the world has to astronomy. Its almanacs are sold to sailors, farmers and the Cyclic Order, who resent needing them |
| [Silent Whisper](#6-the-silent-whisper) | night, and the sun hidden behind the sun at a crossing | meditates through the dimmed hour; keeps tide records at [the Tide Road](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-tide-road), where the moon's rhythm is a discipline of patience |
| [Harmonious Path](#7-the-harmonious-path) | two suns that circle forever and never collide | balance as a fact of the sky; a common teaching image at mediations |
| [Dual Flame](#8-the-dual-flame) | its two flames in the sky: gold that makes, red that unmakes | the second dusk, when only the red sun is up, is the hour for confession and the burning of the page of wrongs |
| [Infinite Compass](#9-the-infinite-compass) | the horizon, where the suns rise further along each season, and the night stars | steers and dates pilgrimages by them; the [Salt Mirror](../Places/Places-of-Interest.md#the-salt-mirror), which turns into a second sky after rain, is its holy site |

**Shared by everyone, whatever their faith** (leans): two shadows are a figure of speech ("he casts two shadows" for a double-dealer); a crossing at a wedding or a signing is lucky or unlucky depending on who you ask; and sailors trust the Circle's almanac and the Order's count less than their own tide post.

**Still open:** the legacy creation myth of a Mother and her three star-Sons, against a world named for two suns ([Open Questions](../Open-Questions.md#faiths)). Possible readings: the myth is older than anyone's sky-watching; the third Son is the one that fell; or the three Sons were never in the sky at all. None is applied.

## Religion and politics

Faiths should not behave like nine political parties.

A member's faith may influence:
- moral language;
- community obligation;
- charitable networks;
- interpretation of events.

But class, region, family, guild, and personal experience also matter.

## Religion and current events

Because these traditions span borders, they are ideal for showing that a crisis in one region can be understood completely differently elsewhere.

Religious networks may also provide the first genuinely trans-regional response to the unfolding political crisis.
