# B6. The collection read

**Goal.** Read Season 1 the way a reader would, in order and in one sitting. Then answer the collection-level questions the story-by-story reviews couldn't: does it work as a book, and what is the shared arc costing it?

**Why.** Every Season 1 story has had its own independent review, and every one passed. Both cold readers of the newer stories raised the same collection-level problem, recorded on [Decisions](../wiki/Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#open-for-the-author): **"a quiet craftsperson with a one-line credo … breaks it by saying one plain thing in public."** The stories differ in engine, register and resolution, but the arc is the same. Only a whole-collection read can say how much that matters, and what the smallest fix would be.

**Done means.** One report, with:
- a reader's account of the collection;
- a verdict on the shared arc;
- the smallest set of changes that would vary it, for bt 7274 to choose from.

## Fixed: don't re-argue

- **The story text.** This brief proposes; bt 7274 writes. [Who writes what](../wiki/Sunday-Morning/Notes/Craft.md#ai-and-the-author-who-writes-what).
- **The Sunday Morning rules** ([Rules](../wiki/Sunday-Morning/Notes/Rules.md)).
- **D21:** connected, not driven. Nobody in a story sees the pattern.

## Read, in this order (about 70,000 words)

1. `AGENTS.md`, `briefs/README.md` and `briefs/STATE.md`.
2. **The fourteen Season 1 drafts in `wiki/Sunday-Morning/Drafts/`,** in the order of [Collection §Reading order](../wiki/Sunday-Morning/Notes/Collection.md#reading-order).
   - Read them straight through, before anything else, and note as you go: where your attention sagged, what you expected next, what repeated, and what you'll remember.
   - Don't open the plan pages, the Registry or the reviews until all fourteen are read. This is a cold read.
3. Only then:
   - `wiki/Sunday-Morning/Notes/Registry.md`, **Story shapes** and **Devices already used**;
   - `wiki/Sunday-Morning/Notes/Collection.md`, **The collection in one sentence** and **How the stories connect**;
   - the shared-arc item on [Decisions](../wiki/Sunday-Morning/Notes/Decisions.md#open-for-the-author);
   - the B3 report, if it exists.

## Cold-read notes, per story

Keep these while reading, before anything else:

```markdown
### <n>. <Title>
- Attention: <where it held, where it sagged>
- Expected next: <what you thought would happen at the turn>
- The turn: <how the story resolves, in one line>
- Echoes: <what felt like an earlier story>
- Will remember: <one image or line>
```

## Questions to answer

1. **As a book.** What does a reader come away with? Where does the collection sag? Which story would they recommend, and which would they skip?
2. **The shared arc.**
   - In how many stories does it actually appear, by your reading, not the record's?
   - Does a reader notice it, and when?
   - Is it a flaw, or the collection's signature?
3. **The smallest fix.** If it needs varying, which two or three stories should change their turn? What kind of turn would each take instead? Name the kind of turn (an object, a refusal, someone else speaking, a quiet private act, a failure), not the prose.
4. **Order.** Does the reading order help or hurt the arc's repetition? Would a different order fix part of it for free?
5. **What's missing.** A register, a kind of protagonist, a kind of ending the collection never offers. Check against **Story shapes**.
